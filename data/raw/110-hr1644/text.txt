 A BILL

To amend the National Labor Relations Act to clarify the definition of 
 ``supervisor'' for purposes of such Act.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Re-Empowerment of Skilled and 
Professional Employees and Construction Tradesworkers (RESPECT) Act''.

SEC. 2. DEFINITION OF SUPERVISOR.

 Section 2(11) of the National Labor Relations Act (29 U.S.C. 
152(11)) is amended--
 (1) by inserting ``and for a majority of the individual's 
 worktime'' after ``interest of the employer'';
 (2) by striking ``assign,''; and
 (3) by striking ``or responsibility to direct them,''.
