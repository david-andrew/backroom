 A BILL

 To restore the second amendment rights of all Americans.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Second Amendment Restoration and 
Protection Act of 1998''.

SEC. 2. REPEAL OF PUBLIC LAW 103-159.

 (a) Repeal.--Public Law 103-159 and each amendment made by such Act 
is repealed, and title 18, United States Code, and section 509 of title 
I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968, shall be 
applied and administered as if Public Law 103-159 had never been 
enacted.
 (b) Interpretive Rule.--Any liability, penalty, or forfeiture 
incurred under Public Law 103-159 or under any amendment made by such 
Act is hereby extinguished, and any action or prosecution for the 
enforcement of any such liability, penalty, or forfeiture shall not be 
sustained.

SEC. 3. REPEAL OF ASSAULT WEAPONS BAN.

 (a) Repeal.--Subtitle A of title XI of the Violent Crime Control 
and Law Enforcement Act of 1994 and each amendment made by such 
subtitle is repealed, and title 18, United States Code, shall be 
applied and administered as if such subtitle had never been enacted.
 (b) Interpretive Rule.--Any liability, penalty, or forfeiture 
incurred under subtitle A of title XI of the Violent Crime Control and 
Law Enforcement Act of 1994 or under any amendment made by such 
subtitle is hereby extinguished, and any action or prosecution for the 
enforcement of any such liability, penalty, or forfeiture shall not be 
sustained.
