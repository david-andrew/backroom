A BILL
To amend the Price-Anderson provisions of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954
to provide for the financial accountability of certain contractors of the
Department of Energy, and for other purposes.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Price-Anderson Financial Accountability
 Amendments of 1989'.
SEC. 2. FINANCIAL ACCOUNTABILITY.
 Section 170 of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 (42 U.S.C. 2210), is amended
 by adding at the end the following new subsection:
 `u. FINANCIAL ACCOUNTABILITY- (1) The Attorney General may bring an action in
 the appropriate United States district court to recover from a contractor
 of the Secretary (or subcontractor or supplier of such contractor) any
 amount paid by the Federal Government under an agreement of indemnification
 under subsection d. for public liability resulting, in whole or in part,
 from the gross negligence or willful misconduct of such contractor (or
 subcontractor or supplier of such contractor).
 `(2) The amount recoverable under paragraph (1) shall not exceed the amount
 of the total contract price received by such contractor (or the amount
 received by such subcontractor or supplier) for the services giving rise
 to such liability in the fiscal year in which such services were performed.
 `(3) The provisions of this subsection shall not be limited by any
 indemnification of persons indemnified under this section.
 `(4) No amount recovered from any contractor (or subcontractor or supplier
 of such contractor) under paragraph (1) may be reimbursed directly or
 indirectly by the Department of Energy.
 `(5)(A) Except as provided in subparagraph (B), no contractor (or
 subcontractor or supplier of such contractor) from whom any amount is
 recovered under paragraph (1) shall be eligible to receive any Federal
 Government contract (or to subcontract or provide supplies with regard
 to any such contract) during the 5-year period beginning on the date on
 which a final judgment awarding such a recovery is made under paragraph (1).
 `(B) The President may exempt a contractor, subcontractor or supplier from
 the provisions of subparagraph (A) if he determines that such an exemption
 is necessary to protect the national security.
 `(6) The Secretary shall, not later than 180 days after the date of the
 enactment of the Price-Anderson Financial Accountability Amendments of
 1989, define by rule, the term `total contract price' for purposes of
 this subsection.'.
SEC. 3. RENEGOTIATION OF CONTRACTS.
 (a) IN GENERAL- During the 1-year period beginning on the date of the
 enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Energy shall, upon the request of
 any contractor subject to an agreement of indemnification under section
 170 d. of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 (42 U.S.C. 2210(d)), enter into
 negotiations with such contractor regarding the terms of compensation of
 the contract subject to such agreement.
 (b) RESCISSION- The provisions of subsection (a) shall not be construed
 to create a right of rescission on the part of any such contractor with
 regard to any such contract.
SEC. 4. EFFECTIVE DATE AND APPLICABILITY.
 (a) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendments made by this Act shall take effect 1
 year after the date of the enactment of this Act.
 (b) APPLICABILITY- The amendments made by this Act shall apply to public
 liability arising from activities occurring on or after the effective date
 of this Act that are covered by an agreement of indemnification under
 section 170 d. of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 (42 U.S.C. 2210(d)),
 regardless of the effective date of such agreement.
