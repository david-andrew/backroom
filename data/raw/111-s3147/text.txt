 A BILL

 To repeal the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Patient Choice Restoration Act''.

SEC. 2. REPEAL.

 The Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act, and the amendments 
made by that Act, are repealed.
