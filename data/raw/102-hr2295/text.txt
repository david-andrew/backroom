HR 2295 IH
102d CONGRESS
1st Session
 H. R. 2295
To disallow the Secretary of the Interior from issuing oil and gas leases
with respect to certain areas of the Outer Continental Shelf located in the
Santa Maria Basin and the Santa Barbara Channel off the coastline of the
State of California.
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
May 9, 1991
Mr. LAGOMARSINO (for himself and Mr. GALLEGLY) introduced the following bill;
which was referred to the Committee on Interior and Insular Affairs
A BILL
To disallow the Secretary of the Interior from issuing oil and gas leases
with respect to certain areas of the Outer Continental Shelf located in the
Santa Maria Basin and the Santa Barbara Channel off the coastline of the
State of California.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. PROHIBITION.
 Notwithstanding section 8 of the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43
 U.S.C. 1337), the Secretary of the Interior--
 (1) may not issue any new oil and gas lease on any submerged lands of the
 Outer Continental Shelf, as such term is defined in section 2(a) of the
 Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1331(a)), located within the
 geographical area described in section 2; and
 (2) may not, before January 1, 2001, issue any new oil and gas lease on any
 submerged lands of the Outer Continental Shelf, as such term is defined in
 section 2(a) of the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1331(a)),
 located within the tracts described in section 3.
SEC. 2. LANDS COVERED BY PERMANENT PROHIBITION.
 The geographical area referred to in section 1(1) is located in the
 Pacific Ocean off the coastline of the State of California and consists
 of the following tracts identified on the official Outer Continental Shelf
 Leasing Map for the Channel Islands Area (map numbered 6B):
 (1) All of tracts numbered 5066, 5067, 5166, 5167, 5168, 5169, 5264, 5265,
 5266, 5267, 5268, and 5269.
 (2) The northwestern quarter of the northwestern quarter of tract numbered
 5165.
 (3) The eastern half and the eastern half of the western half of tract
 numbered 5170.
 (4) The eastern half and the eastern half of the western half of tract
 numbered 5270.
 (5) Any submerged lands within that part of the Channel Islands National
 Marine Sanctuary which lies 3 to 6 miles out from the baseline from which
 the State waters are measured around San Miguel and Prince Islands, Santa
 Rosa, Santa Cruz, Anacapa, and Santa Barbara Islands.
SEC. 3. LANDS COVERED BY TEMPORARY PROHIBITION.
 The tracts referred to in section 1(2) are the 87 tracts in the Santa
 Maria Basin and the Santa Barbara Channel off the coastline of the State of
 California, identified as those to be considered for oil and gas leasing
 under the Draft Proposed Comprehensive Outer Continental Shelf Natural
 Gas and Oil Resource Management Program, 1992-1997, dated February 1991.
