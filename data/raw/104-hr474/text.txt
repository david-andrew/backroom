 A BILL

 To repeal provisions of the Clean Air Act dealing with acid rain.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPEAL OF CLEAN AIR ACT PROVISIONS RELATING TO ACID RAIN.

 Title IV of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7401 and following), 
relating to acid deposition control, is hereby repealed.
