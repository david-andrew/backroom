 A BILL

 To prohibit the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System from 
 making funds available at a discount rate to private individuals, 
 partnerships, and corporations.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPEAL OF DISCOUNT AUTHORITY FOR PRIVATE FIRMS.

 Section 13 of the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. 343) is amended by 
striking the third undesignated paragraph (relating to discounts for 
individuals, partnerships, and corporations).
