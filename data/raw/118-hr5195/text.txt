 A BILL

 To repeal the District of Columbia Home Rule Act.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Seat of Government Act''.

SEC. 2. REPEAL OF DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA HOME RULE ACT.

 The District of Columbia Home Rule Act (sec. 1-201.01 et seq., D.C. 
Official Code) is hereby repealed.
