 A BILL

To provide that imported ethanol shall not count toward satisfaction of 
 any renewable fuel standard that may be enacted.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. DISQUALIFICATION OF IMPORTED ETHANOL FOR THE PURPOSE OF ANY 
 RENEWABLE FUEL STANDARD.

 For the purpose of any renewable fuel standard that may be enacted 
after the date of enactment of this Act, ethanol that is imported, or 
that is derived from any matter that is imported, shall not count 
toward satisfaction of the renewable fuel standard.
