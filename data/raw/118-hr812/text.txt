 A BILL

 To repeal the Inflation Reduction Act of 2022.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Inflation Reduction Act of 2023''.

SEC. 2. REPEAL OF INFLATION REDUCTION ACT OF 2022.

 (a) In General.--Public Law 117-169 (commonly referred to as the 
Inflation Reduction Act of 2022) is repealed.
 (b) Rescissions.--The unobligated balances of any amounts made 
available under Public Law 117-169 are rescinded.
