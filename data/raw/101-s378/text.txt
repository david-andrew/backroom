A BILL
To extend the Steel Import Stabilization Act for an additional five years.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled, That this Act may be cited as the
 `Steel Import Stabilization Extension Act'.
EXTENSION OF ACT
 SEC. 2. Section 806(a) of the Steel Import Stabilization Act (19 U.S.C. 2253
 note) is amended--
 (1) by striking `fifth' in paragraph (1) and inserting in lieu thereof
 `tenth'; and
 (2) by striking `or fourth' in paragraph (2) and inserting in lieu thereof
 `fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth, and ninth'.
