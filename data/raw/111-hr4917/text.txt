 A BILL

 To amend part D of title XVIII of the Social Security Act to prohibit 
 mid-year changes in the formularies of Medicare Part D plans.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. NO MID-YEAR FORMULARY CHANGES PERMITTED.

 (a) In General.--Section 1860D-4(b)(3)(E) of the Social Security 
Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w-104(b)(3)(E)) is amended by inserting ``and only 
at the beginning of a plan year'' before the period.
 (b) Effective Date.--The amendment made by subsection (a) shall 
apply with respect to plan years beginning on or after January 1, 2011.
