 A BILL

To amend the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to exempt agricultural 
 stormwater discharges and silviculture operations from permits under 
 the national pollutant discharge elimination system, and for other 
 purposes.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Timber and Agriculture Environmental 
Fairness Act''.

SEC. 2. NATIONAL POLLUTANT DISCHARGE ELIMINATION SYSTEM.

 Section 402(l) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 
U.S.C. 1342(l)) is amended by adding at the end the following:
 ``(3) Agricultural stormwater discharges.--The 
 Administrator shall not require a permit under this section for 
 discharges composed entirely of agricultural stormwater 
 discharges (including stormwater runoff from cultivated crops, 
 pastures, rangelands, orchards, aquaculture facilities, and 
 forest lands), nor shall the Administrator, directly or 
 indirectly, require any State to require such a permit.
 ``(4) Silviculture operations.--
 ``(A) In general.--The Administrator shall not 
 require a permit under this section for discharges from 
 silviculture operations, nor shall the Administrator, 
 directly or indirectly, require any State to require 
 such a permit.
 ``(B) Silviculture operations defined.--In this 
 section the term `silviculture operation' means the 
 theory and practice of controlling the establishment, 
 composition, constitution, and growth of forests. Such 
 term includes nursery operations, reforestation, 
 cultural treatment, thinning, prescribed burning, 
 harvest operations, site preparation, road construction 
 and maintenance, surface drainage, and pest and fire 
 control.''.

SEC. 3. POINT SOURCE DEFINED.

 Section 502 (14) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 
U.S.C. 1362(14)) is amended by striking the second sentence and 
inserting the following: ``This term does not include agricultural 
stormwater discharges, return flows from irrigated agriculture, and 
discharges of stormwater runoff from silvicultural operations described 
in section 402(l).''.
