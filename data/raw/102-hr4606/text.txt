 H. R. 4606
To direct that the SSN-21 Seawolf attack submarine program be terminated.
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
March 26, 1992
Mr. SANTORUM introduced the following bill; which was referred to the
Committee on Armed Services
A BILL
To direct that the SSN-21 Seawolf attack submarine program be terminated.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. TERMINATION OF SSN-21 SEAWOLF ATTACK SUBMARINE PROGRAM.
 (a) TERMINATION- The Secretary of Defense shall terminate the SSN-21
 Seawolf attack submarine program.
 (b) CONTRACT LIMITATION- No funds may be obligated for a contract for
 construction of an SSN-21 submarine other than the single submarine for which
 a contract was entered into before the date of the enactment of this Act.
