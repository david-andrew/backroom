To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to provide that the minimum

IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
September 30, 1992
Mr. MILLER of California (for himself, Mr. FORD of Michigan, Mr. SANDERS,
Mr. AUCOIN, Mr. SABO, Mr. HAYES of Illinois, Mr. TORRES, Ms. NORTON,
Mr. BERMAN, and Ms. PELOSI) introduced the following bill; which was referred
to the Committee on Education and Labor
A BILL
To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to provide that the minimum
wage rate under that Act will be indexed to the cost of living in the same
manner as Social Security benefits are indexed.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. INDEXING OF MINIMUM WAGE.
 (a) RATE- Section 6(a)(1) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29
 U.S.C. 206(a)(1)) is amended by inserting before the semicolon the following:
 `or the wage rate as determined under subsection (g)'.
 (b) INCREASE- Section 6 of such Act (29 U.S.C. 206) is amended by adding
 at the end the following:
 `(g) At the beginning of each calendar year, the Secretary shall adjust the
 rate applicable under subsection (a)(1) in the same manner as the primary
 insurance amount is adjusted for such calendar year under section 215(i)
 of the Social Security Act.'.
SEC. 2. EFFECTIVE DATE.
 The amendments made by section 1 shall take effect with respect to calendar
 years which begin 1993.
