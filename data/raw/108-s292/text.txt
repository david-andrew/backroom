 A BILL

 To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to exempt licensed 
 funeral directors and licensed embalmers from the minimum wage and 
 overtime compensation requirements of that Act.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. FAIR LABOR STANDARDS ACT OF 1938.

 Section 13(a) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 
213(a)) is amended by inserting after paragraph (3) the following:
 ``(4) any employee employed as a licensed funeral director 
 or a licensed embalmer; or''.
