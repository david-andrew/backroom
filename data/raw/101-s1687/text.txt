IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES
September 28 (legislative day, SEPTEMBER 18), 1989
Mr. BAUCUS introduced the following bill; which was read twice and referred
to the Committee on Finance
A BILL
To repeal section 89 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to rules
for coverage and benefits under certain employee benefit plans).
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. REPEAL OF SECTION 89.
 (a) REPEAL- Sections 1151 of the Tax Reform Act of 1986 and 1011B(a) of
 the Technical and Miscellaneous Revenue Act of 1988 (and the amendments
 made by such sections) are hereby repealed.
 (b) APPLICATION OF INTERNAL REVENUE CODE- The Internal Revenue Code of
 1986 shall be applied and administered as if the provisions described in
 subsection (a) had not been enacted.
