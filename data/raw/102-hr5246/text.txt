A BILL
To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to extend coverage of home
health services under the medicare program.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. EXTENDING COVERAGE OF HOME HEALTH SERVICES UNDER THE MEDICARE
PROGRAM.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Section 1861(m) of the Social Security Act (42
 U.S.C. 1395x(m)) is amended by adding at the end the following new sentence:
 `For purposes of paragraphs (1) and (4) and sections 1814(a)(2)(C)
 and 1835(a)(2)(A), nursing care and home health aide services shall be
 considered to be provided or needed on an `intermittent' basis if they are
 provided or needed less than 7 days each week and, in the case they are
 provided or needed for 7 days each week, if they are provided or needed
 for a period of up to 40 consecutive days.'.
 (b) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to
 services furnished in cases of initial periods of home health services
 beginning on or after January 1, 1993.
SEC. 2. FINANCING THROUGH INCREASE IN DOLLAR LIMITATION ON AMOUNT OF WAGES
SUBJECT TO HOSPITAL INSURANCE TAX.
 Section 3121(x)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended--
 (1) in subparagraph (A), by striking `and' at the end;
 (2) in subparagraph (B)--
 (A) by striking `for any calendar year' and all that follows through
 `preceding year' and inserting `for calendar year 1992, the applicable
 contribution base for calendar year 1991', and
 (B) by striking the period at the end and inserting a comma; and
 (3) by adding at the end the following new subparagraphs:
 `(C) for calendar year 1993, the applicable contribution base for calendar
 year 1992 increased by $5,000 and adjusted in the same manner as is used
 in adjusting the contribution and benefit base under section 230(b) of
 the Social Security Act, and
 `(D) for any calendar year after 1993, the applicable contribution base
 for the preceding year adjusted in the same manner as is used in adjusting
 the contribution and benefit base under section 230(b) of the Social
 Security Act.'.
