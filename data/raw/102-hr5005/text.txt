102d CONGRESS
2d Session
 H. R. 5005
To exempt any person operating a trade or business in the State of Ohio
from all Federal laws and regulations applying with regard to such trade
or business.
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
April 28, 1992
Mr. TRAFICANT introduced the following bill; which was referred to the
Committee on Government Operations
A BILL
To exempt any person operating a trade or business in the State of Ohio
from all Federal laws and regulations applying with regard to such trade
or business.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. EXEMPTION FROM CERTAIN FEDERAL LAWS AND REGULATIONS.
 Any person operating a trade or business in the State of Ohio shall be
 exempt from all Federal laws and regulations applying with regard to such
 a trade or business.
