 A BILL

To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the excise tax on 
 medical devices.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Protect Medical Innovation Act''.

SEC. 2. REPEAL OF MEDICAL DEVICE EXCISE TAX.

 Section 4191 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is hereby 
repealed.
