 A BILL

To amend title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act to eliminate the 
 short-term limited duration insurance exemption with respect to 
 individual health insurance coverage.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Throw Away Junk Plans Act''.

SEC. 2. ELIMINATING THE SHORT-TERM LIMITED DURATION INSURANCE EXEMPTION 
 WITH RESPECT TO INDIVIDUAL HEALTH INSURANCE COVERAGE.

 Section 2791(b)(5) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 
300gg-91(b)(5)) is amended by inserting ``(other than such insurance 
that is issued, sold, or renewed on or after April 1, 2023)'' before 
the period at the end.
