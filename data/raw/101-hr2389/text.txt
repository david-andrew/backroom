Mr. PORTER introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee

A BILL
To amend the Agricultural Act of 1949 to repeal the sugar price support
program.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. REPEAL OF SUGAR PRICE SUPPORT PROGRAM.
 (a) REPEALER- Effective for the 1990 crop of sugar beets and sugarcane,
 section 201 of the Agricultural Act of 1949 (7 U.S.C. 1446) is amended--
 (1) in the matter preceding subsection (b) by striking `sugar beets,
 and sugarcane';
 (2) by striking subsection (j); and
 (3) in subsection (k) by striking `, sugar beets, or sugarcane' each place
 it appears, by striking out the comma and inserting `or' after `peanuts'
 the first and third places it appears, and by striking the comma after
 `soybeans' the first and third places it appears.
 (b) CONFORMING AMENDMENT- Effective for the 1990 crop of sugar beets and
 sugarcane, section 401(e) (7 U.S.C. 1421(e)) is amended by striking `(1)'
 and by striking paragraph (2).
