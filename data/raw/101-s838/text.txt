S. 838
To repeal the estate tax inclusion related to valuation freezes.
IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES
April 19 (legislative day, JANUARY 3), 1989
Mr. HEFLIN introduced the following bill; which was read twice and referred
to the Committee on Finance
A BILL
To repeal the estate tax inclusion related to valuation freezes.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled, That (a) subsection (c) of section
 2036 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (as amended by the Technical
 and Miscellaneous Revenue Act of 1988) is hereby repealed.
 (b) The repeal made by subsection (a) shall apply in the case of property
 transferred after December 17, 1987.
