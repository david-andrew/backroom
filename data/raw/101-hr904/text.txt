A BILL
To extend the Steel Import Stabilization Act for an additional 5 years.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Steel Import Stabilization Extension Act'.
SEC. 2. EXTENSION OF ACT.
 Section 806(a) of the Steel Import Stabilization Act (19 U.S.C. 2253 note)
 is amended--
 (1) by striking out `fifth' in paragraph (1) and inserting `tenth'; and
 (2) by striking out `or fourth' in paragraph (2) and inserting `fourth,
 fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth, or ninth'.
