 A BILL

To amend section 1114 of title 11, United States Code, to preserve the 
 health benefits of certain retired miners.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PRESERVATION OF HEALTH BENEFITS FOR CERTAIN RETIRED MINERS.

 Section 1114 of title 11, United States Code, is amended by adding 
at the end the following:
 ``(n) Notwithstanding any other provision of this section, the 
benefits required to be provided by a last signatory operator under 
chapter 99 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, may not be terminated 
or modified by any court in a proceeding under this title.''.
