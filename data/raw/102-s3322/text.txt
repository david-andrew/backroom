A BILL
To reduce the Federal budget deficit by eliminating entitlement and other
mandatory payments by the United States to wealthy individuals and large
corporations.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. ELIMINATION OF ENTITLEMENT AND MANDATORY PAYMENTS TO WEALTHY
INDIVIDUALS AND LARGE CORPORATIONS.
 The Federal budget deficit is reduced by eliminating entitlement and other
 mandatory payments by the United States to wealthy individuals and large
 corporations as follows:
 (1) No individual shall be eligible to receive any payment from the United
 States Government in any taxable year which would result in the sum of the
 individual's adjusted gross income plus any such payment not already included
 in such adjusted gross income exceeding $120,000 ($60,000 in the case of
 a married individual within the meaning of section 7703 of the Internal
 Revenue Code of 1986 who files a separate return) for such taxable year.
 (2) No person shall be eligible to receive any payment from the United
 States Government in any taxable year which would result in the sum of the
 person's taxable income plus any such payment not already included in such
 taxable income to exceed $120,000 for such taxable year.
 (3) No person who is exempt from tax under chapter 1 of the Internal
 Revenue Code of 1986 shall be eligible to receive any payment from the
 United States Government in any calendar year which would exceed $120,000
 or which would result in the sum of the person's gross receipts plus any
 such payment not already included in such gross receipts to exceed $620,000
 for such calendar year.
 (4) No individual or person who is subject to tax under chapter 3 of the
 Internal Revenue Code of 1986 shall be eligible to receive any payment
 from the United States Government in any calendar year which would exceed
 $120,000 or which would result in the sum of such individual's or such
 person's gross receipts plus any such payment not already included in
 such gross receipts to exceed $620,000 for such calendar year.
SEC. 2. DEFINITIONS.
 For purposes of this Act--
 (1) PERSON- The term `person' means any private entity other than an
 individual, including any estate or trust taxable under section 1(e)
 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
 (2) INDIVIDUAL- The term `individual' includes the individual's spouse and
 dependents as defined in section 152 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
 (3) ADJUSTED GROSS INCOME- The term `adjusted gross income' has the same
 meaning given such term by section 62 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
 (4) TAXABLE INCOME- The term `taxable income' has the same meaning given
 such term by section 63 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
