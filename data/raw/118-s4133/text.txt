 A BILL

 To amend the National Labor Relations Act to require secret ballot 
 elections, and for other purposes.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Protecting Workers from Coercion 
Act''.

SEC. 2. AMENDMENTS TO THE NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS ACT.

 Section 9 of the National Labor Relations Act (29 U.S.C. 159) is 
amended--
 (1) in subsection (a), by striking ``designated or selected 
 for the purposes of collective bargaining'' and inserting ``for 
 the purposes of collective bargaining selected by secret ballot 
 in an election conducted by the Board,''; and
 (2) in subsection (c)--
 (A) in paragraph (1), in the flush matter following 
 subparagraph (B), by inserting ``, not later than 1 
 month after such petition was filed,'' after ``it 
 shall''; and
 (B) by adding at the end the following:
 ``(6) An election may not take place after the filing of 
 any petition until the Board conducts a hearing, and makes a 
 ruling, on--
 ``(A) any issues regarding unit inclusion or 
 exclusion or eligibility of an individual; and
 ``(B) any other legal dispute concerning the 
 election alleged by the employer or a labor 
 organization seeking recognition as the collective 
 bargaining representative of employees of the 
 employer.''.
