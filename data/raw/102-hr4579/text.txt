A BILL
To amend the Clean Air Act to strengthen automobile emission standards.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. LIGHT-DUTY VEHICLE AND LIGHT-DUTY TRUCK STANDARDS.
 Section 202(i) of the Clean Air Act is amended to read as follows:
 `(i) PHASE II STANDARDS FOR LIGHT-DUTY VEHICLES AND LIGHT-DUTY TRUCKS- For
 model years commencing after January 1, 2003, the standards for light-duty
 vehicles and light-duty trucks with a loaded vehicle weight (LVW) of 3,750
 lbs. or less shall not exceed the levels specified in the following table:
table 3--emission standards for light-duty vehicles and light-duty trucks
3,750 lbs. lvw or less
----------------------------
 Pollutant Emission level*
----------------------------
 NMOG 0.04 GPM
 NOx 0.2 GPM
 CO 1.0 GPM
 PM 0.02 GPM
----------------------------
*Emission levels are expressed in grams per mile (GPM). For vehicles and
engines subject to this subsection for purposes of section 202(d) and any
reference thereto, the useful life of such vehicles and engines shall be
a period of 10 years or 100,000 miles (or the equivalent), whichever first
occurs.
