A BILL
To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to deny any deduction for cleanup
and damage costs attributable to certain oil spills in navigable waters.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. DENIAL OF DEDUCTION FOR CLEANUP AND DAMAGE COSTS ATTRIBUTABLE TO
OIL SPILLS IN NAVIGABLE WATERS.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Section 162 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating
 to trade or business expenses) is amended by redesignating subsection
 (m) as subsection (n) and by inserting after subsection (l) the following
 new subsection:
 `(m) DENIAL OF DEDUCTION FOR CLEANUP AND DAMAGE COSTS ATTRIBUTABLE TO
 CERTAIN OIL SPILLS-
 `(1) IN GENERAL- No deduction shall be allowed under this chapter for cleanup
 and damage costs attributable to the discharge from a vessel into navigable
 waters of any oil being transported on such vessel by or for the taxpayer.
 `(2) CLEANUP AND DAMAGE COSTS- For purposes of this subsection, the term
 `cleanup and damage costs' means--
 `(A) any costs incurred in removing or attempting to remove the discharged
 oil or in otherwise reducing the damage from the discharged oil, and
 `(B) any costs attributable to any liability for damages from the
 discharged oil.
 `(3) OIL- The term `oil' means crude oil and any petroleum product.'
 (b) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to
 amounts paid or incurred after December 31, 1988, in taxable years ending
 after such date.
