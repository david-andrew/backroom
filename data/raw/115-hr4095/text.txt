 A BILL

 To repeal Public Law 114-145.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``DEA Opioid Enforcement Restoration 
Act of 2017''.

SEC. 2. REPEAL OF PUBLIC LAW 114-145.

 Public Law 114-145 (the Ensuring Patient Access and Effective Drug 
Enforcement Act of 2016) (including the amendments made by such Act) is 
repealed, and the provisions of sections 303 and 304 of the Controlled 
Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 823, 824) that were amended by Public Law 
114-145 are hereby restored as if Public Law 114-145 had not been 
enacted into law.
