 A BILL

 To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to sunset the limit on 
 the maximum rebate amount for single source drugs and innovator 
 multiple source drugs.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SUNSET OF LIMIT ON MAXIMUM REBATE AMOUNT FOR SINGLE SOURCE 
 DRUGS AND INNOVATOR MULTIPLE SOURCE DRUGS.

 Section 1927(c)(2)(D) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396r-
8(c)(2)(D)) is amended by inserting after ``December 31, 2009,'' the 
following: ``and before January 1, 2020,''.
