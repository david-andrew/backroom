 A BILL

 To prohibit funding to implement any provision of the Patient 
Protection and Affordable Care Act or of the health-related provisions 
 of the Health Care and Education Reconciliation Act of 2010.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``NObamacare Funds Act of 2012''.

SEC. 2. PROHIBITING FUNDING TO IMPLEMENT PUBLIC LAW 111-148 OR THE 
 HEALTH-RELATED PROVISIONS OF PUBLIC LAW 111-152.

 Notwithstanding any other provision of law, on and after the date 
of the enactment of this Act, no funds may be obligated or expended to 
carry out--
 (1) any provision of the Patient Protection and Affordable 
 Care Act (Public Law 111-148), including any amendment made by 
 any such provision; or
 (2) any provision of title I or subtitle B of title II of 
 the Health Care and Education Reconciliation Act of 2010 
 (Public Law 111-152), including any amendment made by any such 
 provision.
