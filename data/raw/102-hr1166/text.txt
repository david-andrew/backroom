IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
February 27, 1991
Mr. SMITH of Florida introduced the following bill; which was referred to
the Committee on Banking, Finance and Urban Affairs
A BILL
To amend the Truth in Lending Act to require certain credit card issuers
who offer grace periods to begin such grace period on the postmark date of
the periodic statement.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Credit Card Grace Period Act of 1991'.
SEC. 2. GRACE PERIOD REQUIRED TO BEGIN ON POSTMARK DATE.
 Section 127 of the Truth in Lending Act (15 U.S.C. 1637) is amended by
 adding at the end the following new subsection:
 `(f) GRACE PERIOD REQUIREMENTS- If the terms of any credit card account
 under an open end consumer credit plan provide that any credit extended
 under such plan may be repaid during a period beginning on the billing
 date of the statement (in which such extension of credit first appears)
 without incurring a finance charge, no date which occurs before the postmark
 date of such statement may be treated as the billing date for purposes of
 determining such period.'.
