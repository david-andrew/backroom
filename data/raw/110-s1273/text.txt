 A BILL

 To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow permanent look-
 through treatment of payments between related foreign corporations.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PERMANENT LOOK-THROUGH TREATMENT OF PAYMENTS BETWEEN RELATED 
 CONTROLLED FOREIGN CORPORATIONS UNDER THE FOREIGN 
 PERSONAL HOLDING COMPANY RULES.

 Section 954(c)(6)(B) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is 
amended by striking ``and before January 1, 2009,''.
