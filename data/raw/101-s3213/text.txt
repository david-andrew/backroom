S. 3213
To clarify that the inspection of meat and poultry products offered for

IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES
October 17 (legislative day, OCTOBER 2), 1990
Mr. DASCHLE introduced the following bill; which was read twice and referred
to the Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry
A BILL
To clarify that the inspection of meat and poultry products offered for
import into the United States is to be conducted by United States personnel,
and for other purposes.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. MEAT INSPECTION.
 Section 20(f) of the Federal Meat Inspection Act (21 U.S.C. 620(f))
 is amended--
 (1) in the first sentence by inserting `same' before `inspection,
 sanitary,'; and
 (2) in the third sentence by inserting `by inspectors of the United States
 before entry for such inspection, sanitary, and quality standard' before
 `for such species verification'.
SEC. 2. POULTRY INSPECTION.
 Section 17(d)(3)(A) of the Poultry Products Inspection Act (21
 U.S.C. 466(d)(3)(A)) is amended by inserting `by inspectors of the United
 States before entry for such inspection, sanitary and quality standards,'
 before `for such species verification'.
