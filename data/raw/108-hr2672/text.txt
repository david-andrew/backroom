 A BILL

 To amend title 23, United States Code, to permit voluntary compliance 
 with provisions relating to the rate of wage paid to laborers and 
 mechanics employed on Federal-aid highway projects.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PREVAILING RATE OF WAGE FOR LABORERS AND MECHANICS EMPLOYED 
 ON FEDERAL-AID HIGHWAY PROJECTS.

 (a) In General.--Section 113 of title 23, United States Code, is 
amended by adding at the end the following:
 ``(d) Sunset Provision.--
 ``(1) In general.--This section shall cease to be effective 
 beginning on the date of enactment of this subsection.
 ``(2) Limitation on statutory construction.--Nothing in 
 this subsection shall be construed to prohibit a State from 
 enacting a State law to provide for continued enforcement of 
 any of the requirements of this section in the State.''.
 (b) Conforming Amendment.--Section 106(d)(1) of title 23, United 
States Code, is amended by striking ``113 or''.
