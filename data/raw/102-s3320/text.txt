To eliminate the price support and production adjustment programs for milk,

A BILL
To eliminate the price support and production adjustment programs for milk,
cotton, rice, honey, and tobacco, and for other purposes.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Agricultural Price Support Elimination Act
 of 1992'.

SEC. 8. TRANSITION PROVISIONS.
 (a) LIABILITY- The amendments made by this Act shall not affect the
 liability of any person under any provision of law as in effect before
 the application of the amendments in accordance with section 9.
 (b) TOBACCO- The Secretary of Agriculture shall issue regulations to
 provide for--
 (1) the orderly disposition of tobacco stocks; and
 (2) the repayment of all tobacco price support loans outstanding on the
 date of enactment of this Act by not later than 1 year after the date of
 enactment of this Act.
SEC. 9. APPLICATION OF AMENDMENTS.
 The amendments made by this Act shall apply beginning with--
 (1) in the case of milk, the calendar year that begins after the date of
 enactment of this Act;
 (2) in the case of upland cotton, extra long staple cotton, rice, and honey,
 the crop year that begins after the date of enactment of this Act; and
 (3) in the case of tobacco, the marketing year that begins after the date
 of enactment of this Act.
