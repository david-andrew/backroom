 A BILL

 To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to adjust the rate 
employers pay for overtime hours from one and one-half to two times the 
 regular rate.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Double the Wage for Overtime Act of 
2026''.

SEC. 2. INCREASING OVERTIME PAY FOR EMPLOYEES.

 (a) In General.--The Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 
201 et seq.) is amended--
 (1) in section 7, by striking ``one and one-half'' each 
 place it appears and inserting ``two''; and
 (2) in section 13--
 (A) in subsection (b)(29), by striking ``one and 
 one-half'' and inserting ``two'';
 (B) in subsection (h), in the matter following 
 paragraph (2)(B), by striking ``one and one-half'' and 
 inserting ``two'';
 (C) in subsection (i)(2)(B), by striking ``one and 
 one-half'' and inserting ``two''; and
 (D) in subsection (j)(2)(B), by striking ``one and 
 one-half'' and inserting ``two''.
 (b) Effective Date.--The amendments made by this Act shall take 
effect on the date that is 180 days after the date of enactment of this 
Act.
