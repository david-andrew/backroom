IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

Mr. WISE introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee
on Education and Labor
A BILL
To provide that certain regulations of the Secretary of Labor relating to
the adjudication of claims under the Black Lung Benefits Act shall be of no
force or effect.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. REGULATIONS.
 The regulations of the Secretary of Labor which are published at 20
 C.F.R. 727.203(b)(3) and (b)(4) are not in accordance with section 402(f)(2)
 of the Black Lung Benefits Act (30 U.S.C. 902(f)(2)) and shall be of no
 force and effect and the Secretary of Labor may not promulgate any other
 regulation which has the same legal effect.
