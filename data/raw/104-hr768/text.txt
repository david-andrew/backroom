 A BILL

 To create a liveable wage by the year 2000.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Liveable Wage Act of 1995''.

SEC. 2. MINIMUM WAGE INCREASE.

 Paragraph (1) of section 6(a) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 
1938 (29 U.S.C. 206(a)) is amended to read as follows:
 ``(1) except as otherwise provided in this section, $4.60 
 during the period beginning January 1, 1996, $4.85 during the 
 period beginning January 1, 1997, $5.00 during the period 
 beginning January 1, 1998, $5.15 during the period beginning 
 January 1, 1999, and $5.30 during the period beginning January 
 1, 2000;''.
