 A BILL

 To codify Executive Order 14248, entitled ``Preserving and Protecting 
 the Integrity of American Elections''.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. CODIFYING EXECUTIVE ORDER RELATING TO PRESERVING AND 
 PROTECTING THE INTEGRITY OF AMERICAN ELECTIONS.

 Executive Order 14248, entitled ``Preserving and Protecting the 
Integrity of American Elections'' (90 Fed. Reg. 14005), shall have the 
force and effect of law.
