 A BILL

 To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to increase the minimum 
 wage.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Better Pay for American Workers 
Act''.

SEC. 2. MINIMUM WAGE INCREASE.

 (a) In General.--Section 6 of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 
(29 U.S.C. 206) is amended--
 (1) in subsection (a), by striking paragraph (1) and 
 inserting the following:
 ``(1) except as otherwise provided in this section, not 
 less than--
 ``(A) $15.00 an hour, beginning on January 1, 2027;
 ``(B) $17.50 an hour, beginning on January 1, 2028;
 ``(C) $20.00 an hour, beginning on January 1, 2029; 
 and
 ``(D) beginning on January 1, 2030, and annually 
 thereafter, the amount determined by the Secretary 
 under subsection (h).''; and
 (2) by adding at the end the following:
 ``(h) Minimum Wage Based on the Median Wage.--
 ``(1) In general.--Prior to the date that a new minimum 
 wage determined under subsection (a)(1)(D) is to take effect, 
 the Secretary shall determine the minimum wage rate to be in 
 effect under this subsection for each year described in 
 subsection (a)(1)(D). The wage rate determined under this 
 subsection for a year shall be--
 ``(A) not less than the amount in effect under 
 subsection (a)(1) on the date of the determination;
 ``(B) increased from such amount by the annual 
 percentage increase, if any, in the median hourly wage 
 of all employees as determined by the Bureau of Labor 
 Statistics;
 ``(C) rounded to the nearest multiple of $0.05, if 
 the amount determined under this subsection is not a 
 multiple of $0.05.
 ``(2) Annual percentage increase.--In calculating the 
 annual percentage increase in such median hourly wage, the 
 Secretary, through the Bureau of Labor Statistics, shall 
 determine the median hourly wage of all employees for the most 
 recent year for which data are available and compare such 
 median hourly wage with the median hourly wage determined for 
 the preceding year.''.
