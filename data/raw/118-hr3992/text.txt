 A BILL

 To amend the Financial Stability Act of 2010 to require stress tests 
 performed pursuant to that Act to evaluate for the increase and 
 decrease of interest rates, and for other purposes.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Effective Bank Regulation Act''.

SEC. 2. STRESS TEST SCENARIO.

 Section 165(i)(1)(B)(i) of the Financial Stability Act of 2010 (12 
U.S.C. 5365(i)(1)(B)(i)) is amended--
 (1) by striking ``2 different'' and inserting ``5 
 different''; and
 (2) by striking ``baseline and severely adverse'' and 
 inserting ``baseline, adverse, severely adverse, a set of 
 conditions that evaluates for increases in interest rates, and 
 a set of conditions that evaluates for decreases in interest 
 rates''.
