HR 2302 IH
101st CONGRESS
1st Session
 H. R. 2302
To amend titles XVIII and XIX of the Social Security Act to repeal the
requirement of nursing facilities to provide for training of nurse aides.
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
May 10, 1989
Mr. GEKAS introduced the following bill; which was referred jointly to the
Committees on Ways and Means and Energy and Commerce
A BILL
To amend titles XVIII and XIX of the Social Security Act to repeal the
requirement of nursing facilities to provide for training of nurse aides.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. REPEAL OF NURSE AIDE TRAINING REQUIREMENT.
 (a) MEDICARE PROGRAM- Section 1819 of the Social Security Act (42
 U.S.C. 1395i-3) is amended--
 (1) by striking paragraph (5) of subsection (b),
 (2) by striking paragraph (1) of subsection (e), and
 (3) by striking paragraph (2) of subsection (f).
 (b) MEDICAID PROGRAM- Section 1919 of the Social Security Act (42
 U.S.C. 1396r) is amended--
 (1) by striking paragraph (5) of subsection (b),
 (2) by striking paragraph (1) of subsection (e), and
 (3) by striking paragraph (2) of subsection (f).
 (c) CONFORMING AMENDMENTS- (1) Section 1903(a)(2) of such Act (42
 U.S.C. 1396b(a)(2)) is amended--
 (A) by striking `(2)(A)' and inserting `(2)', and
 (B) by striking subparagraph (B).
 (2) Section 4211(d) of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1987 is
 amended by striking paragraph (2).
 (d) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendments made by this section shall be effective
 as if included in the enactment of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of
 1987 (Public Law 100-203); except that the amendments made by subsection (c)
 shall apply to calendar quarters beginning after the date of the enactment
 of this Act.
