 A BILL

To amend the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to provide that the 
 same limitation on contributions to candidates shall apply to 
 multicandidate political committees and other persons.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SAME LIMITATION ON CONTRIBUTIONS TO CANDIDATES TO APPLY TO 
 MULTICANDIDATE POLITICAL COMMITTEES AND OTHER PERSONS.
 Section 315(a) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 
U.S.C. 441a(a)) is amended--
 (1) in paragraph (1)(A), by striking out ``$1,000'' and 
 inserting in lieu thereof ``$3,000''; and
 (2) in paragraph (2)(A), by striking out ``$5,000'' and 
 inserting in lieu thereof ``$3,000''.
SEC. 2. EFFECTIVE DATE.

 The amendments made by section 1 shall apply with respect to 
elections taking place more than 6 years after the date of the 
enactment of this Act.
