To repeal the McCarran-Ferguson Act.
IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES
August 2 (legislative day, JULY 8), 1991
Mr. GARN introduced the following bill; which was read twice and referred
to the Committee on the Judiciary
A BILL
To repeal the McCarran-Ferguson Act
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled, That the Act of March 9, 1945
 (59 Stat 33, Chapter 20, 15 U.S.C. 1011 through 1015), commonly known as
 the McCarran-Ferguson Act, is repealed.
