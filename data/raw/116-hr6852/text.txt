 A BILL

To prohibit the Secretary of the Treasury and the Board of Governors of 
the Federal Reserve System from waiving provisions related to COVID-19 
 emergency relief and taxpayer protections related to such relief.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PROHIBITION ON WAIVERS.

 Section 4003(c)(3)(A)(iii) of the CARES Act (15 U.S.C. 
9042(c)(3)(A)(iii)) is amended to read as follows:
 ``(iii) Prohibition on waivers.--The 
 Secretary and the Board of Governors of the 
 Federal Reserve System may not waive any 
 requirement under this section or of a rule 
 issued to carry out this section.''.
