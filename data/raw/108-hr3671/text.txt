 A BILL

To amend part D of title XVIII of the Social Security Act, as added by 
 the Medicare Prescription Drug, Improvement, and Modernization Act of 
 2003, to permit the Secretary of Health and Human Services to enter 
 into direct negotiations to promote best prices for Medicare 
 beneficiaries.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. AUTHORITY TO ENTER INTO NEGOTIATIONS ON PRICES FOR 
 PRESCRIPTION DRUGS FOR MEDICARE BENEFICIARIES.

 Section 1860D-11(i) of the Social Security Act, as added by section 
101(a) of the Medicare Prescription Drug, Improvement, and 
Modernization Act of 2003, is amended to read as follows:
 ``(i) Direct Negotiations.--In order to promote the best price of 
prescription drugs for part D eligible beneficiaries and in carrying 
out this part, the Secretary may enter into direct negotiations 
between--
 ``(1) drug manufacturers and pharmacies; and
 ``(2) PDP sponsors and MA organizations offering MA-PD 
 plans.''.
