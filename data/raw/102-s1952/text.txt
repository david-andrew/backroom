To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 by repealing the provisions

IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES

Mr. HOLLINGS (for himself and Mr. LOTT) introduced the following bill;
which was read twice and referred to the Committee on Finance
A BILL
To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 by repealing the provisions
relating to limitations on passive activity losses and credits.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. REPEAL OF PASSIVE ACTIVITY LOSS LIMITATIONS.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Section 469 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating
 to limitations of passive activity losses and credits) is hereby repealed
 and such Code shall be applied and administered as if such section (and
 provisions relating thereto) had not been enacted.
 (b) CONFORMING AMENDMENTS-
 (1) Section 58 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is hereby repealed.
 (2) Section 55(b)(2)(A) of such Code is amended by striking `and section 58'.
 (3) Section 56(b)(1)(C)(iv) of such Code is amended by striking `sections
 57 and 58' and inserting `section 57'.
 (4) Section 59(h) of such Code is amended by striking `sections 56, 57,
 and 58' and inserting `sections 56 and 57'.
 (5) Section 135(c)(4)(B) of such Code is amended by striking `, 469,'.
 (6) Section 219(g)(3)(A)(i) is amended by striking `sections 86 and 469'
 and inserting `section 86'.
 (7) Section 512(c)(2)(A) of such Code is amended by inserting `as in effect
 on January 1, 1991' in the parenthetical after `section 469(k)(2)'.
 (c) EFFECTIVE DATE-
 (1) IN GENERAL- The amendments made by this section shall apply to passive
 activity losses and passive activity credits arising in taxable years
 beginning after December 31, 1991 (determined without regard to section
 469(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986).
 (2) DEFINITIONS- For purposes of paragraph (1), the terms `passive activity
 loss' and `passive activity credit' have the meanings given such terms in
 section 469(d) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 as in effect on the
 day before the date of the enactment of this Act.
