To amend title 15, United States Code, to authorize the Consumer Product

IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES
April 23 (legislative day, APRIL 9), 1991
Mr. METZENBAUM introduced the following bill; which was read twice and
referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation
A BILL
To amend title 15, United States Code, to authorize the Consumer Product
Safety Commission to regulate the risk of injury associated with firearms.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Gun Safety Act of 1991'.
SEC. 2. FIREARMS.
 Section 3(a)(1)(E) of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15
 U.S.C. 2052(a)(1)(E)) is repealed.
SEC. 3. TECHNICAL AMENDMENT.
 Section 3(a)(1) of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2052(a)(1)) is
 amended by deleting, in the sentence immediately preceding the last sentence,
 the following: `described in subparagraph (E) of this paragraph or'.
SEC. 4. PROHIBITION AGAINST BANNING OF A FIREARM.
 Section 8 of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2057) is amended by
 adding at the end thereof the following new sentence: `This section shall
 not apply in the Commission's regulation of the risk of injury associated
 with firearms.'.
SEC. 5. REPEAL.
 Section 3(e) of the Consumer Product Safety Commission Improvements Act
 of 1976 (15 U.S.C. 2080 note) is repealed.
