 A BILL

 To repeal the sunset on the reduction of capital gains rates for 
individuals and on the taxation of dividends of individuals at capital 
 gain rates.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REDUCTIONS IN INDIVIDUAL CAPITAL GAINS AND DIVIDENDS TAX 
 RATE MADE PERMANENT.

 Section 303 of the Jobs and Growth Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 
2003 (relating to sunset of title) is repealed.
