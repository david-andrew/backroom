 A BILL

To amend the Federal Power Act to require the Federal Energy Regulatory 
 Commission to consider the effects of Commission-jurisdictional rates 
 on the affordability of electricity for consumers, and for other 
 purposes.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Affordable Electricity Rates Act of 
2026''.

SEC. 2. CONSIDERATION OF EFFECTS OF RATES ON CONSUMER AFFORDABILITY.

 Section 201 of the Federal Power Act (16 U.S.C. 824) is amended by 
adding at the end the following:
 ``(h) Affordability as a Component of Just and Reasonable Rates.--
 ``(1) In general.--In making any determination of whether a 
 rate or charge is just and reasonable under this part, the 
 Commission shall--
 ``(A) consider whether the rate or charge is likely 
 to result in retail electricity prices that are 
 unaffordable for electric consumers (as defined in 
 section 3 of the Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act 
 of 1978 (16 U.S.C. 2602)); and
 ``(B) presume that the rate or charge is likely to 
 result in retail electricity prices that are 
 unaffordable for electric consumers (as so defined) if 
 the Commission determines that the rate or charge is 
 likely to result in an increase in retail electricity 
 prices of 5 percent or more.
 ``(2) Unaffordable rates and charges prohibited.--If the 
 Commission determines under paragraph (1) that a rate or charge 
 is likely to result in retail electricity prices that are 
 unaffordable for electric consumers (as defined in section 3 of 
 the Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act of 1978 (16 U.S.C. 
 2602)), such rate or charge shall not be considered to be just 
 and reasonable for purposes of this part.''.
