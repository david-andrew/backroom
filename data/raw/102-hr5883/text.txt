A BILL
To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to increase the minimum wage.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Minimum Wage Amendments of 1992'.
SEC. 2. INCREASE IN MINIMUM WAGE.
 Section 6(a)(1) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 206(a)(1))
 is amended by striking out `and not less than $4.25 an hour after March 31,
 1991' and inserting in lieu thereof `not less than $4.25 an hour until the
 90th day after the date of the enactment of the Minimum Wage Amendments
 of 1992, and not less than $6.50 an hour after the 90th day after such date'.
