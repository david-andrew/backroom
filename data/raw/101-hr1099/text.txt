To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to increase the minimum wage.
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
February 23, 1989
Mr. KLECZKA introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee
on Education and Labor
A BILL
To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to increase the minimum wage.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled, That paragraph (1) of section 6(a)
 of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 206(a)) is amended to
 read as follows:
 `(1) except as otherwise provided in this section, not less than $3.35 an
 hour during the period ending December 31, 1989, not less than $3.85 an
 hour during the year beginning January 1, 1990, and not less than $4.35
 an hour after December 31, 1990;'.
