 A BILL

 To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to reduce the 15 and 28 
percent individual income tax rates to 10 and 23 percent over a 10 year 
 period.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE; ETC.

 (a) Short Title.--This Act may be cited as the ``Tax Rate Reduction 
Act of 1999''.
 (b) Section 15 Not To Apply.--The amendments made by section 2 
shall not be treated as a change in a rate of tax for purposes of 
section 15 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
