 A BILL

To prohibit the Secretary of Education from promulgating regulations or 
guidance regarding gainful employment for purposes of titles I or IV of 
 the Higher Education Act of 1965.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Education for All Act''.

SEC. 2. NO REGULATORY AUTHORITY.

 Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of 
Education may not use any Federal funds for the promulgation of 
regulations or guidance regarding the meaning of the term ``gainful 
employment'' in section 101, 102, or 481 of the Higher Education Act of 
1965 (20 U.S.C. 1001, 1002, 1088).
