 A BILL

 To designate certain lands in Alaska as wilderness.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Morris K. Udall Wilderness Act of 
1993''.

SEC. 2. DESIGNATION OF WILDERNESS AREA.

 In furtherance of the provisions of the Wilderness Act, an area in 
Alaska comprising approximately 1,556,793 acres, as generally depicted 
on a map entitled ``Arctic Coastal Plain Wilderness--Proposed'', dated 
June, 1986, and available for inspection in the offices of the 
Secretary of the Interior, is hereby designated as wilderness and, 
therefore, as a component of the National Wilderness Preservation 
System.
