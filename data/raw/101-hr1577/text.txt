 H. R. 1577
To amend the Clean Air Act to require new stationary sources of air pollutants
which are located in nonattainment areas to periodically reduce emissions.
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
March 22, 1989
Mr. TORRES (for himself and Mr. WAXMAN) introduced the following bill;
which was referred to the Committee on Energy and Commerce
A BILL
To amend the Clean Air Act to require new stationary sources of air pollutants
which are located in nonattainment areas to periodically reduce emissions.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. STATIONARY SOURCES OF AIR POLLUTION IN NONATTAINMENT AREAS.
 Section 173 of the Clean Air Act is amended by inserting `(a) INITIAL PERMIT-
 ' after `SEC. 173.' and by adding the following at the end thereof:
 `(b) PERIODIC REVIEW AND REISSUANCE OF PERMITS- A permit issued under
 subsection (a) after December 31, 1989, shall not remain in effect for
 longer than 5 years after the date on which issued. The permitting agency
 may periodically reissue any such permit for successive terms, each of
 which shall not exceed 5 years. Any permit reissued under this subsection
 shall contain both of the following--
 `(1) Provisions requiring the source to meet conditions and requirements
 at least as stringent as those contained in the expiring permit.
 `(2) Provisions requiring the source to comply with the lowest achievable
 emission rate, determined at the time the owner or operator applies for
 reissuance of the permit.'.
