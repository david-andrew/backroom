A BILL
To amend the Social Security Act to require skilled and other nursing
facilities under the medicare and medicaid programs to provide the services
of a registered nurse for 24 hours per day, 7 days per week.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. 24 HOURS REGISTERED NURSE REQUIREMENT FOR MEDICARE AND MEDICAID
NURSING FACILITIES.
 Part A of title XI of the Social Security Act is amended by adding at the
 end the following new section:
`REQUIRING MEDICARE AND MEDICAID NURSING FACILITIES TO PROVIDE THE SERVICES
OF A REGISTERED NURSE FOR 24 HOURS PER DAY, 7 DAYS PER WEEK
 `SEC. 1140. (a) Notwithstanding any provision of title XVIII or XIX to
 the contrary, except as provided in subsection (b), as a condition of
 participation of a facility as a skilled or other nursing facility under
 title XVIII or a State plan approved under title XIX, the facility must
 use the services of a registered nurse for 24 hours per day, 7 days a week,
 effective as of--
 `(1) January 1, 1991, in the case of a facility with at least 90 beds, or
 `(2) January 1, 1993, in the case of any other facility.
 `(b) The Secretary may waive the requirement of subsection (a) with respect
 to a facility if--
 `(1) the facility demonstrates to the satisfaction of the Secretary that
 the facility has been unable, despite diligent efforts (including offering
 wages at the prevailing rate for registered nurses in the labor market area
 in which the facility is located) to employ qualified registered nurses, and
 `(2) the Secretary finds that the facility has only residents whose
 physicians have indicated (through physicians' orders or admission notes)
 that each such resident does not require registered nursing services during
 the period in which such services are not available.'.
