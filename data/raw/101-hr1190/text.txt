A BILL
To amend chapter 44 of title 18, United States Code, to include semiautomatic
assault weapons and large capacity ammunition feeding devices, and for
other purposes.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Semiautomatic Assault Weapons Act of 1989'.
SEC. 2. CONTROL OF SEMIAUTOMATIC ASSAULT WEAPONS.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Section 922(o) of title 18, United States Code, is amended--
 (1) in paragraph (1), by inserting `or a semiautomatic assault weapon'
 before the period;
 (2) in paragraph (2)--
 (A) in subparagraph (A), by striking `or' the ninth place it appears;
 (B) in subparagraph (B)--
 (i) by inserting `otherwise' before `lawful' each place it appears; and
 (ii) by striking the period and inserting in lieu thereof `; or'; and
 (C) by adding at the end the following:
 `(C) any otherwise lawful transfer or otherwise lawful possession of a
 semiautomatic assault weapon that was lawfully possessed before the date
 this subparagraph takes effect.'.
 (b) SEMIAUTOMATIC ASSAULT WEAPON DEFINED- Section 921(a) of such title is
 amended by adding at the end the following:
 `(25)(A) Except as provided in subparagraph (B), the term `semiautomatic
 assault weapon' means any of the following:
 `(i)(I) Any Avtomat Kalashnikov type semiautomatic firearm.
 `(II) Any UZI type semiautomatic firearm.
 `(III) Any Ingram M-10 or M-11 type semiautomatic firearm.
 `(IV) Any Intratec TEC 9 or 22 type semiautomatic firearm.
 `(V) Any Ruger Mini 14 type semiautomatic firearm.
 `(VI) Any Colt AR-15 type semiautomatic firearm.
 `(VII) Any Beretta AR 70 type semiautomatic firearm.
 `(VIII) Any FN-FAL or FN-FNC type semiautomatic firearm.
 `(IX) Any Steyr A.U.G. type semiautomatic firearm.
 `(X) Any USAS 12 type semiautomatic shotgun.
 `(XI) Any shotgun with a revolving cylinder of the `Street Sweeper' or a
 `Striker 12' type.
 `(XII) Any semiautomatic weapon into which ammunition may be fed by means
 of an ammunition belt or feed strip.
 `(XIII) Any semiautomatic shotgun with a magazine, cylinder, or drum
 capacity exceeding 6 rounds of ammunition.
 `(ii) Any weapon designated as a semiautomatic assault weapon by the
 Secretary in accordance with section 925A.
 `(B) The term `semiautomatic assault weapon' does not include any of
 the following:
 `(i) Any single shot weapon.
 `(ii) Any weapon that--
 `(I) does not use fixed ammunition;
 `(II) was in production before the calendar year 1899;
 `(III) may only be operated manually by means of a bolt, slide, lever,
 or similar action;
 `(IV) is a pistol or rifle with an ammunition feeding device capacity of
 10 or fewer rounds of ammunition;
 `(V) uses rimfire ammunition and employs a tubular magazine; or
 `(VI) uses .22 caliber rimfire ammunition (other than an Intratec TEC 22
 type semiautomatic firearm).
 `(26) The term `semiautomatic', when used with respect to a weapon, means--
 `(A) a weapon which uses a portion of the force of a fired cartridge to
 expel the case of the fired cartridge and load another cartridge into the
 firing chamber, and which requires a separate function of the trigger to
 fire each cartridge; and
 `(B) any part or combination of parts, designed or intended to convert a
 firearm into a weapon described in subparagraph (A), or any combination
 of parts from which a weapon so described may be readily assembled if such
 parts are possessed or controlled by 1 person.'.
 (c) AUTHORITY OF SECRETARY OF THE TREASURY TO DESIGNATE CERTAIN WEAPONS
 AS SEMIAUTOMATIC ASSAULT WEAPONS- Title 18, United States Code, is amended
 by inserting after section 925 the following:
`Sec. 925A. Designation of semiautomatic assault weapons
 `(a) Within 60 days after the date of the enactment of this section, the
 Secretary shall compile and publish a list of weapons designated by the
 Secretary as semiautomatic assault weapons.
 `(b) In determining whether to designate a weapon as a semiautomatic
 assault weapon, the Secretary shall take into account, among other things,
 the following factors:
 `(1) The physical design of the weapon, including--
 `(A) in the case of a rifle or shotgun--
 `(i) whether the weapon has a collapsible or folding stock;
 `(ii) whether the pistol grip facilitates firing the weapon from other
 than a shoulder position; and
 `(iii) whether the length of the barrel is less than 22 inches;
 `(B) in the case of a pistol, whether the ammunition magazine is outside
 the pistol grip; and
 `(C) in the case of any weapon--
 `(i) whether the weapon has a threaded barrel designed to accommodate a
 flash suppressor or silencer; and
 `(ii) whether the weapon is designed to accept a bayonet or barrel shroud.
 `(2) Whether the weapon was originally designed or has been used for
 military, combat, or law enforcement purposes, or is similar in design or
 use to a weapon so designed or used.
 `(3) The degree to which the weapon is similar in design to any of the
 weapons listed in section 921(a)(25)(A)(i).
 `(4) Whether the weapon was originally designed for fully automatic fire.
 `(5) In the case of a rifle or pistol, whether the manufacturer of the
 weapon sells or distributes a magazine with a capacity of 11 or more rounds
 of ammunition produced specifically for use with the weapon.
 `(6) Whether a kit is available to convert the weapon to a fully automatic
 weapon.
 `(c) The Secretary shall designate a weapon (other than the weapons described
 in section 921(a)(25)(B)) as a semiautomatic assault weapon if the weapon
 is substantially similar in design, function, and use to a weapon listed
 in section 921(a)(25)(A)(i).
 `(d) After the publication of the list pursuant to subsection (a), the
 Secretary may periodically, in accordance with subsections (b) and (c),
 designate weapons (other than the weapons described in section 921(a)(25)(B))
 to be semiautomatic assault weapons.'.
 (c) CONFORMING AMENDMENTS-
 (1) SECRETARY OF THE TREASURY NOT REQUIRED TO AUTHORIZE IMPORTATION OF
 SEMIAUTOMATIC ASSAULT WEAPONS- Section 925(d)(3) of such title is amended
 by inserting `, is not a semiautomatic assault weapon,' before `and is
 generally recognized'.
 (2) TRANSPORTATION OF SEMIAUTOMATIC ASSAULT WEAPONS IN INTERSTATE OR
 FOREIGN COMMERCE BY UNLICENSED PERSONS; SALE AND DELIVERY TO UNLICENSED
 PERSONS- Paragraph (4) of each of sections 922(a) and 922(b) of such
 title is amended by inserting `semiautomatic assault weapon,' before
 `short-barreled shotgun,'.
 (3) ENHANCED PENALTIES FOR POSSESSION OR USE OF SEMIAUTOMATIC ASSAULT
 WEAPON DURING CRIME OF VIOLENCE OR DRUG TRAFFICKING CRIME- Section 924(c)(1)
 of such title is amended by inserting `or a semiautomatic assault weapon'
 after `machinegun' each place it appears.
SEC. 3. CONTROL OF LARGE CAPACITY AMMUNITION FEEDING DEVICES.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Section 922 of title 18, United States Code, is amended
 by adding at the end the following:
 `(q)(1) It shall be unlawful for any person to transfer or possess a
 prohibited ammunition feeding device.
 `(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to the otherwise lawful possession of
 an ammunition feeding device that was lawfully possessed in the United
 States on the day before the effective date of this subsection.'.
 (b) PROHIBITED AMMUNITION FEEDING DEVICE DEFINED- Section 921(a) of such
 title is amended by adding after the paragraphs added by section 2(b)
 of this Act the following:
 `(27) The term `prohibited ammunition feeding device' means--
 `(A) a detachable magazine, drum, belt, feed strip, or similar device
 which has a capacity of, or can be readily restored or converted to accept,
 more than 10 rounds of ammunition;
 `(B) any combination of parts--
 `(i) designed and intended solely and exclusively for assembling a device
 described in subparagraph (A); and
 `(ii) from which a device described in subparagraph (A) could be assembled
 if such parts were possessed or controlled by 1 person; and
 `(C) any part specifically designed for use in assembling a device described
 in subparagraph (A).'.
 (c) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendments made by subsections (a) and (b) shall
 apply to conduct engaged in 60 or more days after the date of the enactment
 of this Act.
SEC. 4. APPLICATION OF FIREARMS TAXES AND REGISTRATION TO SEMIAUTOMATIC
ASSAULT WEAPONS.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Subsection (a) of section 5845 of the Internal Revenue Code
 of 1986 (defining firearm) is amended by striking `and (8) a destructive
 device' and inserting `(8) a destructive device; and (9) a semiautomatic
 assault weapon (as defined in section 921(a)(25) of title 18, United
 States Code)'.
 (b) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect
 on the 60th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
