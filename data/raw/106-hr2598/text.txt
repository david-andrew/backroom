 A BILL

 To terminate the price support and marketing quota programs for 
 peanuts.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. TERMINATION OF PRICE SUPPORT AND MARKETING QUOTA PROGRAMS 
 FOR PEANUTS.

 (a) Price Support.--Section 155 of the Agricultural Market 
Transition Act (title I of Public Law 104-127; 7 U.S.C. 7271) is 
repealed.
 (b) Marketing Quota.--Part VI of subtitle B of title III of the 
Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 (7 U.S.C. 1357-1359a), relating to 
peanuts, is repealed.
 (c) Conforming Amendments.--The Agricultural Act of 1949 (7 U.S.C. 
1441 et seq.) is amended--
 (1) in section 101(b) (7 U.S.C. 1441(b)), by striking ``and 
 peanuts''; and
 (2) in section 408(c) (7 U.S.C. 1428(c)), by striking 
 ``peanuts,''.
 (e) Prohibition on Subsequent Provision of Price Support.--
 (1) Prohibition.--After the effective date of this section, 
 the Secretary of Agriculture may not make price support 
 available, whether in the form of loans, purchases, or other 
 operations, to peanut producers for the 2000 and subsequent 
 crops of peanuts by using the funds of the Commodity Credit 
 Corporation or under the authority of any law.
 (2) Outstanding loans.--Notwithstanding paragraph (1), the 
 Secretary shall settle any outstanding loans under section 155 
 of the Agricultural Market Transition Act (title I of Public 
 Law 104-127; 7 U.S.C. 7271) made before the effective date of 
 this section with regard to peanuts.
 (f) Continued Liability of Producers.--An amendment made by this 
section shall not affect the liability of any person under any 
provision of law as in effect before the effective date of this 
section.
 (g) Effective Date.--The amendments made by this section shall take 
effect October 1, 1999, and shall apply with respect to the 2000 and 
subsequent crops of peanuts.
