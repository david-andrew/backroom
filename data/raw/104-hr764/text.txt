 A BILL

 To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to increase the minimum 
 wage.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Fair Labor Standards Amendments of 
1995''.

SEC. 2. MINIMUM WAGE INCREASE.

 Section 6(a)(1) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 
206(a)(1)) is amended to read as follows:
 ``(1) except as otherwise provided in this section, not 
 less than $4.25 an hour during the period ending 6 months after 
 the date of the enactment of the Fair Labor Standards 
 Amendments of 1995, $4.50 an hour during the succeeding year, 
 $4.75 during the succeeding year, $5.00 during the succeeding 
 year, and $5.25 thereafter;''.
