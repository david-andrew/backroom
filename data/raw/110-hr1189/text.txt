 A BILL

 To preserve the right to habeas corpus.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as ``The Habeas Corpus Preservation Act''.

SEC. 2. HABEAS CORPUS.

 Nothing in the Military Commissions Act of 2006 or any amendment 
made by that Act shall affect the right of any resident of the United 
States of America to habeas corpus. The preceding sentence shall be 
construed in accordance with the 5th and 14th Amendments to the 
Constitution of the United States.
