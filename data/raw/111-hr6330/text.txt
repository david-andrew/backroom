 A BILL

 To make permanent the reduced individual income tax rate on dividends.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Preserving American Income on 
Dividends Act of 2010''.

SEC. 2. REDUCED INDIVIDUAL INCOME TAX RATE ON DIVIDENDS MADE PERMANENT.

 The Jobs and Growth Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2003 (relating 
to sunset of title) is amended by striking section 303.
