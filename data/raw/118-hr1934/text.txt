 A BILL

To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Department of 
 Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Salaries and Expenses for fiscal year 
 2024.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. LIMITATION ON AVAILABILITY OF FUNDS FOR DEPARTMENT OF LABOR, 
 WAGE AND HOUR DIVISION, SALARIES AND EXPENSES FOR FISCAL 
 YEAR 2024.

 Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized 
to be appropriated or otherwise available for Department of Labor, Wage 
and Hour Division, Salaries and Expenses for fiscal year 2024 may not 
exceed $229,000,000.
