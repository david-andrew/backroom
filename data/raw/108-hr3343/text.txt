 A BILL

 To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to reduce the highest rate 
 of income tax for corporations.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REDUCTION IN HIGHEST RATE OF INCOME TAX FOR CORPORATIONS.

 (a) In General.--Paragraph (1) of section 11(b) of the Internal 
Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking subparagraphs (C) and (D), 
by inserting ``and'' at the end of subparagraph (B), and by inserting 
after subpargraph (B) the following new subparagraph:
 ``(C) 30 percent (32 percent in the case of taxable 
 years beginning in 2004, 2005, or 2006) of so much of 
 the taxable income as exceeds $75,000.''.
 (b) Effective Date.--The amendments made by this section shall 
apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2003.
