 A BILL

To require the Secretary of Homeland Security to cease all immigration 
 detention operations at the Delaney Hall Detention Center in Newark, 
 New Jersey, and for other purposes.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Delaney Hall Closure Act''.

SEC. 2. DEFINITIONS.

 In this Act:
 (1) Department.--The term ``Department'' means the 
 Department of Homeland Security.
 (2) Secretary.--The term ``Secretary'' means the Secretary 
 of Homeland Security.

SEC. 3. CLOSURE OF DELANEY HALL DETENTION CENTER.

 (a) In General.--The Secretary--
 (1) not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment 
 of this Act, shall cease all immigration detention operations 
 at the Delaney Hall Detention Center in Newark, New Jersey; and
 (2) shall not enter into any contract or agreement to 
 operate the Delaney Hall Detention Center in Newark, New 
 Jersey, as a facility to detain aliens in the custody of the 
 Secretary.
 (b) Termination for Convenience.--
 (1) In general.--In the Government's interest, the 
 Secretary shall terminate any contract for the operation of the 
 Delaney Hall Detention Center in Newark, New Jersey.
 (2) Liability.--The Department shall be liable only for 
 payment under the payment provisions of a contract described in 
 paragraph (1) for services rendered before the date on which 
 the termination of such contract becomes effective.
