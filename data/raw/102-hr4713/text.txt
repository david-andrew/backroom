IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

Mr. RHODES introduced the following bill; which was referred jointly to the
Committees on Ways and Means and Government Operations
A BILL
To repeal the fee and tax increases contained in the Omnibus Budget
Reconciliation Act of 1990.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. REPEAL OF FEE AND TAX INCREASES CONTAINED IN 1990 BUDGET ACT.
 Effective on and after the date of the enactment of this Act--
 (1) titles X and XI of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1990 are
 hereby repealed, and
 (2) all provisions of law shall be applied and administered as if such
 titles (and the amendments made by such titles) had not been enacted.
