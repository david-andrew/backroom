 A BILL

 To repeal the Service Contract Act of 1965.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPEAL.

 The Service Contract Act of 1965 (41 U.S.C. 351 et seq.) is 
repealed.

SEC. 2. APPLICATION.

 The amendment made by section 1 shall not apply to a contract which 
was entered into before the date of the enactment of this Act and to 
which the Service Contract Act of 1965 applied.
