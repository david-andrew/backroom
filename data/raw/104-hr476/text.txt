 A BILL

 To amend the Clean Air Act to repeal certain emissions standards for 
 motor vehicles which have not yet taken effect.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPEAL OF CERTAIN MOTOR VEHICLE EMISSION STANDARDS.

 Section 202 of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7401 and following) is 
amended as follows:
 (1) Subsection (a)(1) is amended by adding at the end 
 thereof the following: ``For all motor vehicles in any class or 
 category manufactured after the model year 1995, the standard 
 promulgated by the Administrator under this section shall be 
 the standard applicable to such class or category of motor 
 vehicle in the model year 1995.''.
 (2) Subsection (a)(3)(B)(ii) is repealed.
 (3) Subsection (h) is repealed.
 (4) Subsection (i) is repealed.
