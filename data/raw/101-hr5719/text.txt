IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
September 26, 1990
Mr. KOLTER introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee
on Energy and Commerce
A BILL
To require generators of hazardous waste to treat or dispose of their waste
at the site on which the waste was generated.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. REQUIREMENT.
 (a) REQUIREMENT- Each generator of hazardous waste shall perform--
 (1) treatment of that waste so as to render the waste harmless; or
 (2) disposal or recycling of that waste;
on the site on which the waste was generated.
 (b) EXCEPTION- The requirement of subsection (a) shall not apply to
 generators who generate waste in a small quantity, as that term is used
 in the Solid Waste Disposal Act.
 (c) DEFINITIONS- For purposes of this Act, the terms `generator', `hazardous
 waste', `treatment', and `disposal' have the meanings given those terms
 in section 1004 of the Solid Waste Disposal Act (42 U.S.C. 6903).
