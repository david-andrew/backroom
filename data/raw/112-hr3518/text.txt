 A BILL

 To impose a regulatory moratorium and prevent taxes from being raised 
 for 2 years.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. IN GENERAL.

 During the period beginning on the date of enactment of this Act 
and terminating on the date that is 2 years after that date, no agency 
may make any rule that would affect employment levels, and the Congress 
shall not enact any law resulting in an increase in the tax liability 
of any person or entity.
