 A BILL

To amend the Fair Credit Reporting Act to require the disclosure of all 
information in a consumer's file, including credit scores, risk scores, 
 and any other predictors.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Fair Credit Full Disclosure Act''.

SEC. 2. ALL INFORMATION IN CONSUMER'S FILE REQUIRED TO BE DISCLOSED.

 Section 609(a)(1) of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. 
1681g(a)(1)) is amended to read as follows:
 ``(1) All information in the consumer's file at the time of 
 the request, including any information concerning credit scores 
 or any other risk scores or predictors relating to the 
 consumer.''.
