 A BILL

 To amend the Agricultural Adjustment Act to exempt actions undertaken 
 to administer a marketing order issued under such Act from the 
 antitrust laws.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. EXEMPTION FROM ANTITRUST LAWS FOR AGRICULTURE MARKETING 
 ORDERS.

 Section 10 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act (7 U.S.C. 610), 
reenacted with amendments by the Agricultural Marketing Agreement Act 
of 1937, is amended by adding at the end the following:
 ``(k) No action, regulation, decision, determination, or other act 
or omission of any individual acting on behalf of, or under the 
authority of, the Secretary of Agriculture with respect to an 
agricultural marketing order or agreement issued under the authority of 
section 8c or another provision of law shall be construed to be within 
the prohibitions of the antitrust laws of the United States.''.
