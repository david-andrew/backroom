 A BILL

To amend title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act to include short-
 term limited duration plans in the definition of individual health 
 insurance coverage.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Stop Junk Health Plans Act of 
2018''.

SEC. 2. PREVENTING JUNK PLANS AND CONTINUING PROTECTIONS FOR CONSUMERS 
 WITH PREEXISTING CONDITIONS.

 (a) Including Short-Term Limited Duration Insurance as Individual 
Health Insurance Coverage.--Section 2791(b)(5) of the Public Health 
Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg-91(b)(5)) is amended by striking ``but 
does not include short-term limited duration insurance'' and inserting 
``including short-term limited duration insurance''.
 (b) Effective Date.--The amendments made by this section shall 
apply with respect to plan years beginning after December 31, 2018.
