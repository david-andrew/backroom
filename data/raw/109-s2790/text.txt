 A BILL

To repeal the fossil fuel energy tax incentives contained in the Energy 
 Policy Act of 2005.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPEAL OF 2005 ENERGY ACT FOSSIL FUEL ENERGY TAX INCENTIVES.

 (a) Repeal.--The provisions of, and the amendments made by, 
subtitle B of title XIII of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 are repealed 
and the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 shall be applied and administered 
as if such provisions and amendments had never been enacted.
 (b) Effective Date.--This section shall take effect as if the 
provisions described in subsection (a) had never been included in the 
Energy Policy Act of 2005.
