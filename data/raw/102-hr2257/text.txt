A BILL
To repeal the tax increases contained in the Revenue Reconciliation Act
of 1990.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled, That the following provisions
 of the Revenue Reconciliation Act of 1990 are hereby repealed (and the
 Internal Revenue Code of 1986 shall be applied and administered as if such
 provisions and the amendments made by such provisions had not been enacted):
 (1) Part I of subtitle A (relating to provisions affecting high-income
 individuals).
 (2) Subtitle B (relating to excise taxes).
 (3) Subtitle C (relating to other revenue increases).
The preceding sentence shall not apply in the case of any tax imposed before
the date of the enactment of this Act by reason of the amendments made by
subtitle B of the Revenue Reconciliation Act of 1990.
