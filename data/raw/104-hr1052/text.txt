 A BILL

 To amend the Clean Air Act to repeal the reformulated gasoline 
 provisions and the provisions relating to work-related vehicle trip 
 reduction, and for other purposes.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPEAL OF CERTAIN CLEAN AIR PROVISIONS.

 (a) Reformulated Gasoline.--Section 211(k) of the Clean Air Act 
(relating to reformulated gasoline) is hereby repealed.
 (b) Employee Trip Reduction.--Section 182(d)(1)(B) of such Act is 
repealed and section 108(f)(1)(A) of such Act is amended by striking 
clause (iii).
