 A BILL

 To make permanent the reduction in taxes on dividends and capital 
 gains.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Investment Tax Increase Prevention 
Act of 2004''.

SEC. 2. PERMANENT REDUCTION IN TAXES ON DIVIDENDS AND CAPITAL GAINS.

 Section 303 of the Jobs and Growth Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 
2003 (relating to sunset of title) is repealed.
