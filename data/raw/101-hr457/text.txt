A BILL
To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the estate and gift
taxes and the tax on generation-skipping transfers.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled, That (a) chapter 11 of the Internal
 Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to estate tax) is hereby repealed.
 (b) Chapter 12 of such Code (relating to gift tax) is hereby repealed.
 (c) Chapter 13 of such Code (relating to tax on generation-skipping
 transfers) is hereby repealed.
 (d)(1) The repeal made by subsection (a) shall apply to the estates of
 decedents dying after the date of the enactment of this Act.
 (2) The repeals made by subsections (b) and (c) shall apply with respect
 to transfers made after the date of the enactment of this Act.
