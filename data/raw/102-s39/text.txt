A BILL
To amend the National Wildlife Refuge Administration Act.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States
 of America in Congress assembled, That notwithstanding any provisions of
 the National Wildlife Refuge Administration Act, a portion of the Arctic
 National Wildlife Refuge in Alaska comprising approximately 1,556,793
 acres, as generally depicted on a map entitled `Arctic Coastal Plain
 Wilderness--Proposed', dated June, 1986, and available for inspection in
 the offices of the Secretary of the Interior, is hereby designated as a
 component of the National Wilderness Preservation System.
