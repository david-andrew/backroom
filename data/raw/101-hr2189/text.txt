TITLE I--AMENDMENTS TO THE FEDERAL ELECTION CAMPAIGN ACT OF 1971
SEC. 101. DEFINITION OF QUALIFYING HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES CANDIDATE.
 Section 301(19) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2
 U.S.C. 431(19)) is amended to read as follows:
 `(19) The term `qualifying House of Representatives candidate' means a
 candidate for the office of Representative in, or Delegate or Resident
 Commissioner to, the Congress, whose principal campaign committee includes
 in its statement of organization a declaration of intention under section
 303(b)(7) and, by reason of such declaration, is subject to the expenditure
 limitations specified in section 315(i) or section 315(j).'.
SEC. 102. REGISTRATION AS QUALIFYING HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES CANDIDATE.
 Section 303(b) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 433(b))
 is amended--
 (1) in paragraph (5), by striking out `and' after the semicolon at the end;
 (2) in paragraph (6), by striking out the period at the end and inserting
 in lieu thereof the following: `; and'; and
 (3) by adding at the end the following:
 `(7) in the case of a principal campaign committee of a candidate for the
 office of Representative in, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the
 Congress, who desires to be a qualifying House of Representatives candidate,
 a declaration of intention of the candidate to use broadcast time under
 section 315(b)(3) of the Communications Act of 1934 or to receive reduced
 postal rates under section 3629 of title 39, United States Code.'.
SEC. 103. LIMITATIONS ON EXPENDITURES BY QUALIFYING HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
CANDIDATES.
 Section 315 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 441a)
 is amended by adding at the end the following new subsections:
 `(i) A qualifying House of Representatives candidate shall not make
 expenditures derived from personal funds of such candidate in excess of
 $50,000 with respect to an election for the office of Representative in,
 or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress.
 `(j)(1) Except as provided in paragraph (2), a qualifying House
 of Representatives candidate shall not make expenditures in excess of
 $200,000 with respect to an election for the office of Representative in,
 or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress.
 `(2) If any candidate in an election referred to in paragraph (1) (other
 than a qualifying House of Representatives candidate) receives contributions
 or makes expenditures aggregating more than 50 percent in excess of the
 limitation applicable to a qualifying House of Representatives candidate
 under that paragraph, the limitation under that paragraph shall not apply
 to any candidate in the election.'.
SEC. 104. EXCLUSION OF CERTAIN NEWSPAPER ADVERTISING RATE REDUCTIONS FROM
THE DEFINITION OF THE TERM `CONTRIBUTION'.
 Section 301(8)(B) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2
 U.S.C. 431(8)(B)) is amended--
 (1) by striking out `and' after the semicolon in clause (xiii);
 (2) by striking out the period at the end of clause (xiv) and inserting
 in lieu thereof `; and'; and
 (3) by adding at the end the following new clause:
 `(xv) the value of any advertising rate reduction made available to a
 qualifying House of Representatives candidate by a newspaper or magazine, if
 such reduction is made available to any qualifying House of Representatives
 candidate and such rate reduction is made available during the 90-day
 period before the election involved.'.
SEC. 105. LIMITATION ON CANDIDATE POLITICAL COMMITTEES.
 Section 302 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 432)
 is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection:
 `(j) A candidate for Federal office may not establish, maintain, or control
 a political committee, other than an authorized committee of the candidate
 or a committee of a political party.'.
SEC. 106. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 313 OF THE FEDERAL ELECTION CAMPAIGN ACT OF
1971 TO ELIMINATE THE EXCESS CAMPAIGN FUND GRANDFATHER PROVISION.
 Section 313 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 439a)
 is amended by striking out `, with respect to ' and all that follows through
 `1979,'.
SEC. 107. STRICTER LIMITATION ON MULTICANDIDATE POLITICAL COMMITTEE
CONTRIBUTIONS TO CANDIDATES.
 Section 315(a)(2)(A) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2
 U.S.C. 441a(a)(2)(A)) is amended by striking out `$5,000' and inserting
 in lieu thereof `$1,000'.
SEC. 108. INTERMEDIARY OR CONDUIT AMENDMENTS.
 Section 315(a) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2
 U.S.C. 441a(a)) is amended--
 (1) by redesignating paragraph (8) as paragraph (8)(A);
 (2) in the first sentence of paragraph (8)(A), as so redesignated by
 paragraph (1), by striking out `For purposes' and inserting in lieu thereof
 `Except as provided in subparagraphs (B) and (C), for purposes';
 (3) in the second sentence of paragraph (8)(A), as so redesignated by
 paragraph (1), by striking out `The' and inserting in lieu thereof `In
 addition to any other report required by law, the'; and
 (4) by adding at the end the following new subparagraphs:
 `(B) Except as provided in subparagraph (C), a contribution made by a
 person through an intermediary or conduit, as described in subparagraph
 (A), shall be treated as a contribution by that person and as a contribution
 by the intermediary or conduit, if--
 `(i) the contribution is in the form of a check or other negotiable
 instrument made payable to the intermediary or conduit; or
 `(ii) the intermediary or conduit is a political committee, an officer,
 employee, or other agent of such a political committee, or an officer,
 employee, or other agent of a connected organization acting in its behalf.
 `(C) Subparagraph (B) shall not apply to--
 `(i) bona fide joint efforts conducted solely for the purpose of sponsorship
 of a fundraising reception, dinner, or other event by (I) two or more
 candidates, (II) two or more national, State, or local committees of a
 political party acting on their own behalf, or (III) a special committee
 formed by two or more candidates or by one or more candidates and one or
 more national, State, or local committees of a political party acting on
 their own behalf; or
 `(ii) fundraising efforts for the benefit of a candidate which are conducted
 by another candidate.'.
SEC. 109. LIMITATION ON ACCEPTANCE OF NONPARTY MULTICANDIDATE POLITICAL
COMMITTEE CONTRIBUTIONS BY HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES CANDIDATES.
 Section 315 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 441a),
 as amended by section 103 of this Act, is further amended by adding at
 the end the following new subsection:
 `(k)(1) A candidate for the office of Representative in, or Delegate or
 Resident Commissioner to, the Congress, and the authorized political
 committees of such candidate, may not accept any contribution from a
 nonparty multicandidate political committee with respect to--
 `(A) a general or special election (including any primary election relating
 to such general or special election) for such office which exceeds $100,000
 ($125,000 if at least two candidates qualify for the ballot in the general or
 special election and at least two candidates qualify for the ballot in the
 primary election) when added to the total of contributions previously made
 by nonparty multicandidate political committees to such candidate and his
 political committees with respect to such general or special election; and
 `(B) a runoff election for such office which exceeds $40,000 when added
 to the total of contributions previously made by nonparty multicandidate
 political committees to such candidate and his authorized political
 committees with respect to such runoff election.
 `(2) As used in this subsection, the term `nonparty multicandidate political
 committee' means a multicandidate political committee, as defined in
 subsection (a)(4), other than a multicandidate political committee of a
 political party.'.
SEC. 110. INDEPENDENT EXPENDITURE BROADCAST REQUIREMENTS.
 Section 318(a) of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2
 U.S.C. 441d(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
 `(c)(1) Whenever any person makes an independent expenditure through
 a broadcast communication on any television station, the broadcast
 communication shall include a statement clearly readable to the viewer
 that appears continuously during the entire length of the broadcast
 communication setting forth the name of such person (and, in the case of
 a political committee, the name of any connected organization).
 `(2) Whenever any person makes an independent expenditure through a
 newspaper, magazine, outdoor advertising facility, direct mailing or
 other type of general public political advertising, the communication
 shall include, in addition to the other information required by this
 subsection, that such person paid for the communication, and in the case
 of a political committee, the name of any connected organization and the
 name of the president or treasurer of such organization, and the following:
 `The cost of presenting this communication is not subject to any campaign
 contribution limits'.'.
SEC. 111. BROADCAST ADVERTISING REQUIREMENTS FOR HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
CANDIDATES.
 Title III of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 431 et
 seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
`BROADCAST ADVERTISING REQUIREMENTS FOR HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES CANDIDATES
 `SEC. 324. Notwithstanding any other provision of law--
 `(1) any radio advertisement of a candidate for the office of Representative
 in, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress shall include
 a voice identifying the candidate and stating: `[Name of candidate] has
 reviewed and approved this advertisement.'.; and
 `(2) any television advertisement of a candidate for the office of
 Representative in, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress
 shall include a continuous and clearly readable statement as follows:
 `[Name of candidate] has reviewed and approved this advertisement.'.
SEC. 112. MULTICANDIDATE POLITICAL COMMITTEE TO MAKE CONTRIBUTIONS TO ONLY
ONE CANDIDATE IN EACH HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES GENERAL ELECTION.
 Section 315 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 441a),
 as amended by sections 103 and 109 of this Act, is further amended by
 adding at the end the following new subsection:
 `(l) A multicandidate political committee (including any affiliated
 multicandidate political committee) may not make contributions to more than
 one candidate in a general election for the office of Representative in,
 or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress. As used in this
 section the term `multicandidate political committee' has the meaning
 given that term in subsection (a)(4).'.
SEC. 113. MULTICANDIDATE POLITICAL COMMITTEE TO MAKE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
GENERAL ELECTION CONTRIBUTIONS ONLY IN ELECTION YEARS.
 Section 315 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 441a),
 as amended by sections 103, 109, and 112 of this Act, is further amended
 by adding at the end the following new subsection:
 `(m) A multicandidate political committee may make contributions with
 respect to a general election for the office of Representative in, or
 Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress only in the year of
 the election. As used in this section the term `multicandidate political
 committee' has the meaning given that term in subsection (a)(4).'.
SEC. 114. DESIGNATION OF RECIPIENT OF MULTICANDIDATE POLITICAL COMMITTEE
CONTRIBUTIONS BY ORIGINAL CONTRIBUTOR.
 Title III of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 431 et
 seq.), as amended by section 111 of this Act, is further amended by adding
 at the end the following new section:
`DESIGNATION OF RECIPIENT OF CERTAIN MULTICANDIDATE POLITICAL COMMITTEE
CONTRIBUTIONS BY ORIGINAL CONTRIBUTOR
 `SEC. 325. (a) Any person making a contribution to a multicandidate political
 committee with respect to a general election for the office of Representative
 in, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress shall have the
 right to designate that the committee use the amount contributed to support
 (1) any specific candidate, or (2) any candidate of a specific political
 party, in the State of residence of the person making the contribution. If
 the person making the contribution does not make such a designation, the
 multicandidate political committee shall use at least 50 percent of the
 amount contributed in support of a candidate in the congressional district
 of residence of the person making the contribution.
 `(b) Amounts used under this subsection shall be subject to limitation as
 if such amounts were contributions by the original contributor directly to
 the candidate or an authorized committee of the candidate. If application of
 subsection (a) would exceed such a limitation, the multicandidate political
 committee may use the excess amount as otherwise provided by law.
 `(c) As used in this section, the term `multicandidate political committee'
 has the meaning given that term in section 315(a)(4).'.

SEC. 204. AMENDMENTS TO TITLE 39, UNITED STATES CODE, RELATING TO POSTAL
RATES FOR CERTAIN ELECTION MATERIALS.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Subchapter II of chapter 36 of title 39, United States Code,
 is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
`Sec. 3629. Reduced rates for certain House of Representatives candidates
 `(a) The rates of postage for matter mailed with respect to a campaign
 by a qualifying House of Representatives candidate (as defined in section
 301 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971) shall--
 `(1) in the case of first-class mail matter, be one-half of the rates
 currently in effect under former section 4253 of this title; and
 `(2) in the case of third-class mail matter, be the same as the rates of
 postage for a qualified political committee under section 3626 of this title.
 `(b) The reduced rates provided under subsection (a) of this section shall
 be available only with respect to matter mailed during the 90-day period
 ending on the day before the date of the election involved.'.
 (b) AMENDMENT TO DEFINITION OF QUALIFIED POLITICAL COMMITTEE FOR CERTAIN
 RATE REDUCTIONS- Section 3626(e)(2) of title 39, United States Code,
 is amended to read as follows:
 `(2) As used in this subsection, the term `qualified political
 committee' means the principal campaign committee of a qualifying House
 of Representatives candidate (as defined in section 301 of the Federal
 Election Campaign Act of 1971).'.
 (c) CONFORMING AMENDMENTS- (1) Section 2401(c) of title 39, United States
 Code, is amended by striking out `and 3626(a)-(h) of this title,' and
 inserting in lieu thereof `3626(a)-(h), and 3629 of this title'.
 (2) Section 3627 of title 39, United States Code, is amended by striking
 out `or 3626 of this title,' and inserting in lieu thereof `3626, or 3629
 of this title'.
 (d) TECHNICAL AMENDMENT- The table of sections for chapter 36 of title 39,
 United States Code, is amended by inserting after the item relating to
 section 3628 the following new item:
`3629. Reduced rates for certain House of Representatives candidates.'.
SEC. 205. CREDIT FOR CONTRIBUTIONS TO QUALIFYING HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
CANDIDATES.
 (a) GENERAL RULE- Subpart A of part IV of subchapter A of chapter 1 of the
 Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to nonrefundable personal credits)
 is amended by inserting after section 23 the following new section:
`SEC. 24. CONTRIBUTIONS TO QUALIFYING HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES CANDIDATES.
 `(a) GENERAL RULE- In the case of an individual, there shall be allowed,
 subject to the limitations of subsection (b), as a credit against the tax
 imposed by this chapter for the taxable year, an amount equal to the full
 amount of all House of Representatives candidate contributions, as defined
 in subsection (c)(1).
 `(b) LIMITATIONS-
 `(1) MAXIMUM CREDIT- The credit allowed by subsection (a) for a taxable
 year shall not exceed $100 ($200 in the case of a joint return under
 section 6013).
 `(2) VERIFICATION- The credit allowed by subsection (a) shall be allowed,
 with respect to any House of Representatives candidate contribution, only if
 the person claiming the credit submits a signed statement attesting to the
 amount and date of said contribution, the office sought by the recipient,
 and such other information as the Secretary may require by regulation,
 except that such other information shall not include the recipient's
 political affiliation, name, or other identifying information.
 `(3) CONTRIBUTIONS MADE THROUGH INTERMEDIARY ORGANIZATIONS- The credit
 under subsection (a) shall not be allowed, with respect to a House of
 Representatives candidate contribution, if the contribution is transmitted
 to the candidate or a campaign committee of the candidate through an
 intermediary group, organization, or committee.
 `(c) DEFINITIONS- For purposes of this section--
 `(1) HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES CANDIDATE CONTRIBUTION- The term `House of
 Representatives candidate contribution' means a contribution or gift of
 money, payment of which is made during the taxable year, to an individual
 who is a a qualifying House of Representatives candidate (as defined in
 section 301 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971), and which--
 `(A) is from a taxpayer (or either spouse in case of a joint return) who is
 a resident of the congressional district in which the election is held; and
 `(B) is solely for use by the recipient to further his candidacy for
 nomination or election to such office.
 `(2) CANDIDATE- The term `candidate' means an individual who--
 `(A) publicly announces before the close of the calendar year following
 the calendar year in which the contribution or gift is made that he is a
 candidate for nomination or election to one of the offices specified in
 paragraph (1); and
 `(B) meets the qualifications prescribed by law to hold such office.
 `(d) CREDIT NOT ALLOWED TO ESTATES OR TRUSTS- No credit shall be allowed
 under this section to any estate or trust.'
 (b) CLERICAL AMENDMENT- The table of sections for subpart A of part IV of
 subchapter A of chapter 1 of such Code is amended by inserting after the
 item relating to section 23 the following new item:
`Sec. 24. Contributions to qualifying House of Representatives candidates.'.
 (c) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendments made by this section shall apply to
 contributions paid after December 31, 1989, in taxable years ending after
 that date.
