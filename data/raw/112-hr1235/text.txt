 A BILL

 To provide a Federal regulatory moratorium, and for other purposes.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Regulation Moratorium Act of 2011''.

SEC. 2. FEDERAL REGULATORY MORATORIUM.

 Except as provided in section 3, no department or agency of the 
United States shall put into force any rule until January 31, 2013.

SEC. 3. EXCEPTIONS TO MORATORIUM.

 Section 2 does not apply in the case of a rule excepted by section 
553(a) from the requirements of that section or for which notice of 
proposed rulemaking is not required under subsection 553(b).
