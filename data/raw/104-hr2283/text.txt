 A BILL

 To prohibit the regulation of the sale or use of tobacco or tobacco 
 products by the Secretary of Health and Human Services.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. PROHIBITION.

 (a) In General.--The Secretary of Health and Human Services may not 
regulate the sale or use of tobacco or tobacco products under the 
Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and may not take any action with 
respect to tobacco or tobacco products under the Federal Cigarette 
Labeling and Advertising Act or the Comprehensive Smokeless Tobacco 
Health Education Act of 1986 which was not authorized before the date 
of the enactment of this Act.
 (b) Regulations.--Any regulation issued by the Secretary of Health 
and Human Services relating the regulation of the sale or use of 
tobacco or tobacco products which takes effect before the date of the 
enactment of this Act shall on such date be considered as repealed.
 (c) Construction.--This Act shall not be construed as a statement 
that the Secretary of Health and Human Services had the authority to 
regulate the sale or use of tobacco or tobacco products before the date 
of the enactment of this Act.
