 A BILL

 To repeal provisions of the Clean Air Act dealing with toxic air 
 emissions.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPEAL OF CLEAN AIR ACT PROVISIONS RELATING TO TOXIC AIR 
 EMISSIONS.

 Section 112 of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7401 and following), 
relating to hazardous air pollutants, is hereby repealed.
