A BILL
To provide equal access to Presidential, congresssional, and judicial records.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Equal Access to Presidential, Congressional,
 and Judicial Records Act'.
SEC. 2. ACCESS TO RECORDS.
 Section 552 of title 5, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:
 `(f) Notwithstanding section 551 of this title, for purposes of this
 section the term `agency' includes--
 `(1) any establishment in the executive branch of the Government, including--
 `(A) the Executive Office of the President;
 `(B) the President;
 `(C) the Vice President;
 `(D) any Executive agency;
 `(E) any military department; and
 `(F) any Government controlled corporation;
 `(2) any independent regulatory agency;
 `(3) any establishment in the legislative branch of the Government,
 including--
 `(A) the House of Representatives; and
 `(B) the Senate; and
 `(4) the Administrative Office of the United States Courts.'.
