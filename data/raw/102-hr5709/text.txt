102d CONGRESS
2d Session
 H. R. 5709
To eliminate the Medicare peer review system.
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
July 29, 1992
Mr. DUNCAN introduced the following bill; which was referred jointly to the
Committees on Ways and Means and Energy and Commerce
A BILL
To eliminate the Medicare peer review system.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. ELIMINATION OF PEER REVIEW SYSTEM.
 Part B of title XI of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1320c et seq.) is
 repealed.
