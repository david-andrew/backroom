 A BILL

 To reinstate all financial assistance awards terminated by the 
 Department of Energy.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Stop the Trump Electricity Price 
Hikes Act''.

SEC. 2. REINSTATEMENT OF TERMINATED AWARDS.

 Notwithstanding any other provision of law, all financial 
assistance awards terminated by the Department of Energy pursuant to 
the Secretarial Memorandum entitled ``Ensuring Responsibility for 
Financial Assistance,'' dated May 15, 2025, shall be deemed reinstated 
and shall continue in effect as if such terminations had not occurred.
