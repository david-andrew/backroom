 A BILL

To reinstate the safeguard measures imposed on imports of certain steel 
 products, as in effect on December 4, 2003.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REINSTATEMENT OF SAFEGUARD MEASURES ON CERTAIN STEEL 
 PRODUCTS.

 Notwithstanding the proclamation of the President of the United 
States of December 4, 2003--
 (1) those safeguard measures implemented under Proclamation 
 7529 of March 5, 2002, with respect to certain steel products, 
 including the modifications made by Proclamation 7529 to 
 subchapter III of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule 
 of the United States, shall remain in effect during the period 
 beginning on December 5, 2003, and ending on the applicable 
 date provided in subchapter III of chapter 99 of such Schedule, 
 as in effect on December 4, 2003; and
 (2) the proclamation of the President of December 4, 2003, 
 and the modifications to the Harmonized Tariff Schedule made by 
 that proclamation, shall have no effect.
