 A BILL

To amend the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to exempt agricultural 
 stormwater and silviculture operation discharges from the requirement 
for a permit under the national pollutant discharge elimination system, 
 and for other purposes.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Timber and Agriculture Environmental 
Fairness Act''.

SEC. 2. EXEMPTION FOR AGRICULTURAL STORMWATER AND SILVICULTURE 
 OPERATION DISCHARGES.

 (a) In General.--Section 402(l) of the Federal Water Pollution 
Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1342(l)) is amended by adding at the end the 
following:
 ``(3) Agricultural stormwater discharges.--The 
 Administrator shall not require a permit under this section, 
 nor shall the Administrator directly or indirectly require any 
 State to require such a permit, for discharges composed 
 entirely of agricultural stormwater discharges, including 
 stormwater runoff from--
 ``(A) cultivated crops;
 ``(B) pastures;
 ``(C) rangeland;
 ``(D) orchards;
 ``(E) aquaculture facilities; and
 ``(F) forest land.
 ``(4) Silviculture operation.--
 ``(A) Definition of silviculture operation.--
 ``(i) In general.--In this paragraph, the 
 term `silviculture operation' means the 
 practice of controlling the establishment, 
 composition, constitution, and growth of 
 forests.
 ``(ii) Inclusions.--The term `silviculture 
 operation' includes--
 ``(I) a nursery operation;
 ``(II) reforestation;
 ``(III) cultural treatment;
 ``(IV) thinning;
 ``(V) prescribed burning;
 ``(VI) a harvest operation;
 ``(VII) site preparation;
 ``(VIII) road construction and 
 maintenance;
 ``(IX) surface drainage; and
 ``(X) pest and fire control.
 ``(B) Permit requirement.--The Administrator shall 
 not require a permit under this section, nor shall the 
 Administrator directly or indirectly require any State 
 to require such a permit, for discharges from a 
 silviculture operation.''.
 (b) Conforming Amendment.--Section 502(14) of the Federal Water 
Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1362(14)) is amended by striking the 
second sentence and inserting the following: ``The term does not 
include agricultural stormwater discharges, return flows from irrigated 
agriculture, and discharges of stormwater runoff from silviculture 
operations (as defined in section 402(l)(4)).''.
