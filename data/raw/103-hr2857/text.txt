 A BILL

 To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the credit 
 provided by section 936 of such Code.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, That (a) section 936 of 
the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to Puerto Rico and 
possession tax credit) is hereby repealed.
 (b) The table of sections for subpart D of part III of subchapter N 
of chapter 1 of such Code is amended by striking the item relating to 
section 936.
 (c) The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable 
years beginning after December 31, 1993.
