 A BILL

 To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to increase the minimum 
 wage.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. MINIMUM WAGE INCREASE.

 (a) Amendment.--Section 6(a)(1) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 
1938 (29 U.S.C. 206(a)(1)) is amended to read as follows:
 ``(1) except as otherwise provided in this section, not 
 less than $4.75 an hour;''.
 (b) Effective Date.--The amendment made by subsection (a) shall 
take effect on the first day of the 3rd month beginning after the date 
of the enactment of this Act.
