 H. R. 3615
To amend the Solid Waste Disposal Act to require treatment of medical waste.
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
November 8, 1989
Mr. LIPINSKI (for himself, Mrs. COLLINS, Mr. HAYES of Illinois, Mr. HYDE,
Mr. POSHARD, Mr. SAVAGE, Mr. SANGMEISTER, and Mr. YATES) introduced the
following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Energy and Commerce
A BILL
To amend the Solid Waste Disposal Act to require treatment of medical waste.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Medical Waste Treatment Amendments of 1989'.
SEC. 2. MEDICAL WASTE TREATMENT.
 Subtitle J of the Solid Waste Disposal Act (42 U.S.C. 6992b et seq.) is
 amended--
 (1) by striking the subtitle heading and inserting the following:
`Subtitle J--Medical Waste
`Part 1--DEMONSTRATION MEDICAL WASTE TRACKING PROGRAM';
 (2) by striking `this subtitle' each place it appears in sections 11001
 through 11012 and inserting `this part'; and
 (3) by adding at the end the following new part:
`Part 2--Treatment of Medical Waste
`SEC. 11021. REGULATORY OPTIONS.
 `(a) IN GENERAL- The Administrator shall regulate the treatment of medical
 waste using one of the following options:
 `(1) ISSUANCE OF REGULATIONS- The Administrator shall, not later than
 30 days after submission of the final report under section 11008(a),
 issue regulations--
 `(A) to define `infectious components of medical waste'; and
 `(B) to specify the procedures and standards for compliance to be used in
 rendering infectious components of medical waste noninfectious.
 `(2) LISTING MEDICAL WASTE AS A HAZARDOUS WASTE- If the Administrator
 does not issue regulations by such date under paragraph (1), he shall list
 medical waste as a hazardous waste under section 3001(b)(1).
 `(b) CONSEQUENCES-
 `(1) IF REGULATIONS ARE ISSUED- In the event that the Administrator issues
 regulations under subsection (a)(1), sections 11022 through 11024 shall
 apply to the regulation of medical waste.
 `(2) IF MEDICAL WASTE LISTED AS HAZARDOUS WASTE- In the event that the
 Administrator lists medical waste as a hazardous waste under subsection
 (a)(2), such waste shall be regulated as a hazardous waste under subtitle
 C and sections 11022 through 11024 shall have no legal effect.
`SEC. 11022. ONSITE TREATMENT.
 Not later than 30 days after the issuance of regulations under section
 11021(a)(1), all generators of medical waste who produce more than 50
 pounds per month of medical waste shall render all infectious components
 of such waste noninfectious in accordance with such regulations before
 the waste leaves the site of its production.
`SEC. 11023. ENFORCEMENT.
 `(a) CRIMINAL PENALTIES- Any person who knowingly violates the requirements
 of section 11022 or regulations issued under section 11021(a)(1), shall,
 upon conviction, be subject to a fine of not more than $50,000 for each day
 of violation, or imprisonment not to exceed 5 years. If the conviction is
 for a violation committed after a first conviction of such person under
 this paragraph, the maximum punishment shall be doubled with respect to
 both fine and imprisonment.
 `(b) KNOWING ENDANGERMENT- Any person who knowingly violates the requirements
 of section 11022 or regulations issued under section 11021(a)(1), who
 knows at that time that he thereby places another person in imminent danger
 of death or serious bodily injury, shall upon conviction be subject to a
 fine of not more than $250,000 or imprisonment for not more than 15 years,
 or both. A defendant that is an organization shall, upon conviction under
 this subsection, be subject to a fine of not more than $1,000,000. The
 terms of this subsection shall be interpreted in accordance with the rules
 provided under section 3008(f) of this Act.
 `(c) CIVIL PENALTIES- Any person who violates any requirement of section
 11022 or regulations issued under section 11021(a)(1) shall be liable to
 the United States for a civil penalty in an amount not to exceed $25,000
 for each such violation. Each day of such violation shall, for purposes
 of this section, constitute a separate violation.
 `(d) CIVIL PENALTY POLICY- Civil penalties assessed by the United States
 or by the States under this part shall be assessed in accordance with the
 Administrator's `RCRA Civil Penalty Policy', as such policy may be amended
 from time to time.
`SEC. 11024. RELATIONSHIP TO STATE LAW.
 `Nothing in this part shall--
 `(1) preempt any State or local law, except to the extent such law conflicts
 with the requirements of section 11022 or regulations issued under section
 11021(a)(1); or
 `(2) otherwise affect any State or local law or the authority of any State
 or local government to adopt or enforce any State or local law so long
 as such law does not conflict with the requirements of section 11022 or
 regulations issued under section 11021(a)(1).'.
SEC. 3. CONFORMING AMENDMENTS TO THE SOLID WASTE DISPOSAL ACT.
 (a) DEFINITION- Section 1004(40) of the Solid Waste Disposal Act (42
 U.S.C. 6903(40)) is amended--
 (1) by striking out `hazardous waste identified or listed under subtitle
 C or any'; and
 (2) by adding after the final sentence the following new sentence: `Such
 term shall include those wastes listed under paragraphs (1) through (11)
 of section 11002(a) as well as all other wastes identified but not excluded
 by the Administrator during the demonstration program established under
 part 1 of subtitle J.'.
 (b) TABLE OF CONTENTS- The table of contents for the Solid Waste Disposal
 Act is amended--
 (1) by striking `Subtitle J--Demonstration Medical Waste Tracking Program'
 and inserting
`Subtitle J--Medical Waste
`PART 1--DEMONSTRATION MEDICAL WASTE TRACKING PROGRAM'; and
 (2) by adding after the item relating to section 11012 the following
 new items:
`PART 2--Treatment of Medical Waste
`Sec. 11021. Regulatory options.
`Sec. 11022. Onsite treatment.
`Sec. 11023. Enforcement.
`Sec. 11024. Relationship to State law.'.
SEC. 4. EFFECTIVE DATE.
 The amendment made by section 3(a) shall take effect 30 days after submission
 of the final report under section 11008(a).
