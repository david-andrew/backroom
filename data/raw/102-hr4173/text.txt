SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Rebuild the American Automobile Industry Act'.
TITLE I--MORATORIUM ON THE IMPORTATION, AND THE DOMESTIC SALE, OF CERTAIN
MOTOR VEHICLES
SEC. 101. DEFINITIONS.
 As used in this title:
 (1) The term `entered' means entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for
 consumption, in the United States.
 (2) The term `domestic corporation' has the meaning given such term by
 section 7701 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
 (3) The term `domestic motor vehicle manufacturer' means a domestic
 corporation that manufactures motor vehicles in one or more production
 facilities located in the United States.
 (4) The term `HTS' means the Harmonized Tariff Schedules of the United
 States.
 (5) The term `moratorium period' means calendar years 1996, 1997, 1998,
 1999, and 2000.
 (6) The term `motor vehicle' means any article of a kind described in
 heading 8703 or 8704 of the HTS.
 (7) The term `reporting corporation' has the meaning given such term in
 section 6038A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
 (8) The term `Secretary' means the Secretary of Commerce.
 (9) The term `transplanted motor vehicle manufacturer' means a domestic
 motor vehicle manufacturer that is also a reporting corporation.
 (10) The term `United States' means the States, the District of Columbia,
 and the commonwealth of Puerto Rico.
SEC. 102. MORATORIUM ON THE IMPORTATION OF CERTAIN MOTOR VEHICLES.
 (a) IN GENERAL- The following articles may not be entered at any time
 during the moratorium:
 (1) Any motor vehicle that is--
 (A) a product of Japan; or
 (B) treated as a product of Japan under subsection (b).
 (2)(A) Except as provided in subparagraph (B), any motor vehicle that--
 (i) is manufactured by or for a domestic motor vehicle manufacturer in
 a production facility that is not located within the United States or
 Japan; and
 (ii) would, if permitted to be entered, be marketed by such manufacturer
 within the United States.
 (B) Subparagraph (A) does not include any motor vehicle that is manufactured
 within the European Community by a subsidiary of a domestic corporation
 if such corporation acquired the subsidiary on or after January 1, 1986.
 (b) TREATMENT OF CERTAIN MOTOR VEHICLES AS PRODUCTS OF JAPAN-
 (1) IN GENERAL- The Secretary of the Treasury shall also treat as motor
 vehicles that are products of Japan those motor vehicles manufactured in a
 foreign country other than Japan with respect to which the Secretary finds
 that the vehicles were manufactured in such foreign country in facilities
 of a corporation or other legal entity that is owned or controlled by--
 (A) natural persons who are citizens of Japan, or
 (B) another corporation or other legal entity that is owned or controlled
 by natural persons who are citizens of Japan.
 (2) OWN OR CONTROL- For purposes of this subsection, the term `own or
 control' means--
 (A) in the case of a corporation, the holding of at least 50 percent
 (by vote or value) of the capital structure of the corporation; and
 (B) in the case of any other kind of legal entity, the holding of interests
 representing at least 50 percent of the capital structure of the entity.
SEC. 103. RESTRICTION ON DOMESTIC MARKETING OF CERTAIN MOTOR VEHICLES.
 (a) DEFINITIONS- For purposes of this section--
 (1) The term `domestic motor vehicle parts producer' means a domestic
 corporation that--
 (A) is not a reporting corporation; and
 (B) manufactures motor vehicle parts in one or more facilities located in
 the United States.
 (2) The term `motor vehicle parts' means--
 (A) articles of a kind described in any of headings 8707 and 8708 and
 subheadings 8407.3100, 8408.20, 8409, 8483, 8706.00.10, and 8706.00.15 of
 the HTS; and
 (B) any other article (described by classification under the HTS) that the
 Secretary considers appropriate as a motor vehicle part for the purposes
 of this title.
 (b) PROHIBITED SALES- Except as provided in subsection (c), during the
 moratorium period it is unlawful to sell, or to offer for sale, in interstate
 commerce any motor vehicle manufactured within the United States within
 such period by a domestic motor vehicle manufacturer or transplanted motor
 vehicle manufacturer unless all motor vehicle parts used in the production
 of such vehicle were produced by domestic motor vehicle parts producers
 in production facilities located within the United States.
 (c) EXCEPTION- The prohibition in subsection (b) shall not apply with
 respect to the sale, or offer for sale, or offer for sale, in interstate
 commerce of motor vehicles of a particular model if the Secretary finds
 that the production by domestic motor vehicle parts producers within the
 United States of one or more kinds of motor vehicle parts that are used
 in manufacture of such model is not adequate to sustain the manufacture
 of such model during the model year.
 (d) CIVIL PENALTIES- Any person who knowingly violates subsection (b) or any
 rule or regulation issued under section 104, shall be liable, after notice
 and opportunity for a hearing in accordance with section 554 of title 5,
 United States Code, and subject to subsection (e), to the United States
 for a civil penalty of not more than $25,000. Each day of a continuing
 violation under this subsection shall constitute a separate offense. The
 Secretary may compromise, modify, or remit, with or without conditions,
 any penalty that is subject to imposition or that has been imposed for a
 violation under this subsection.
 (e) PROCEDURES FOR ASSESSING, AND REVIEW OF, CIVIL PENALTIES-
 (1) NOTICE- Before issuing an order assessing a civil penalty against any
 person for a violation under subsection (d), the Secretary shall provide
 to such person written notice of the proposed penalty.
 (2) ASSESSMENT- The Secretary shall promptly assess such penalty, by
 order, after the day of the receipt of the notice under paragraph (1)
 of the proposed penalty.
 (3) ENFORCEMENT OF ASSESSMENT- If the penalty has not been paid within 90
 calendar days after the assessment order has been made under paragraph (2),
 the Secretary shall institute an action in the appropriate district court
 of the United States for an order affirming the assessment of the civil
 penalty. The court shall have authority to review de novo the law and the
 facts involved, and shall have jurisdiction to enter a judgment enforcing,
 modifying, and enforcing as so modified or setting aside in whole or in
 part, such assessment.
 (4) RECOVERY IF ASSESSMENT NOT PAID- If any person fails to pay an
 assessment of a civil penalty after the appropriate district court has
 entered final judgment in favor of the Secretary under paragraph (3),
 the Secretary shall institute an action to recover the amount of such
 penalty in any appropriate district court of the United States. In such
 action, the validity and appropriateness of such final assessment order
 or judgment shall not be subject to review.
SEC. 104. REGULATIONS.
 The Secretary shall prescribe such regulations that are necessary or
 appropriate to carry out this title.
TITLE II--AMENDMENTS OF INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1986
SEC. 201. EXCISE TAXES CERTAIN ACTIVITIES OF DOMESTIC MOTOR VEHICLE
MANUFACTURERS.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Chapter 36 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating
 to certain other excise taxes) is amended by adding at the end thereof
 the following new subchapters:
`Subchapter G--Profits of Transplanted Motor Vehicle Manufacturers Transferred
to Japan
`Sec. 4501. Imposition of tax.
`SEC. 4501. IMPOSITION OF TAX.
 `(a) IN GENERAL- There is hereby imposed a tax on any direct or indirect
 transfer of the profits of any transplanted motor vehicle manufacturer
 to any citizen or resident of Japan (including any corporation created or
 organized under Japanese law).
 `(b) AMOUNT OF TAX- The amount of the tax imposed by subsection (a) shall
 be 100 percent of the amount of the transfer.
 `(c) TRANSPLANTED MOTOR VEHICLE MANUFACTURER- For purposes of this section,
 the term `transplanted motor vehicle manufacturer' means any domestic
 corporation which is also a reporting corporation (within the meaning of
 section 6038A).
 `(d) APPLICATION OF SECTION- This section shall apply during the period
 beginning on January 1, 1996, and ending on December 31, 2000.
`Subchapter H--Profits of Domestic Motor Vehicle Manufacturers Not Reinvested,
Etc.
`Sec. 4506. Imposition of tax.
`SEC. 4506. IMPOSITION OF TAX.
 `(a) IN GENERAL- There is hereby imposed a tax on the expenditure of any of
 the profits of any domestic motor vehicle manufacturer for a nonqualified
 purpose.
 `(b) AMOUNT OF TAX- The amount of the tax imposed by subsection (a) shall
 be 100 percent of the amount of the expenditure.
 `(c) NONQUALIFIED PURPOSE- For purposes of this section--
 `(1) IN GENERAL- The term `nonqualified purpose' means any purpose other
 than research and development, retooling factories, or retraining workers.
 `(2) INCREASES IN WAGES AND DIVIDENDS IN EXCESS OF RATE OF INFLATION
 TREATED AS NONQUALIFIED-
 `(A) WAGES- Any increase in remuneration of employees shall be treated as
 a payment of profits for a nonqualified purpose to the extent the rate
 of such increase exceeds the rate of the increase in the Consumer Price
 Index since the most recent prior increase in such remuneration.
 `(B) DIVIDENDS- Any increase in dividends shall be treated as a payment of
 profits for a nonqualified purpose to the extent the rate of such increase
 (as compared to the average amount of dividends paid during the preceding
 3 years) exceeds the average rate of the increase in the Consumer Price
 Index for such 3-year period.
 `(3) EXCEPTION FOR PURPOSES SPECIFIED BY THE SECRETARY OF COMMERCE- The
 term `nonqualified purpose' shall not include any purpose specified by
 regulation by the Secretary of Commerce.
 `(d) DOMESTIC MOTOR VEHICLE MANUFACTURER- For purposes of this section, the
 term `domestic motor vehicle manufacturer' means a domestic corporation
 that manufactures motor vehicles in one or more production facilities
 located in the United States. Such term does not include any reporting
 corporation (within the meaning of section 6038A).
 `(e) APPLICATION OF SECTION- This section shall apply during the period
 beginning on January 1, 1996, and ending on December 31, 2000.'
 (b) CLERICAL AMENDMENT- The table of subchapters for chapter 36 of such
 Code is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new items:
`Subchapter G. Profits of Transplanted Motor Vehicle Manufacturers Transferred
to Japan.
`Subchapter H. Profits of Domestic Motor Vehicle Manufacturers Not Reinvested,
Etc.'
 (c) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendments made by this section shall take effect
 on January 1, 1996.
SEC. 202. DENIAL OF DEDUCTION FOR PAYMENTS OF EXCESSIVE COMPENSATION BY
DOMESTIC MOTOR VEHICLE MANUFACTURERS.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Section 162 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating
 to deduction for trade or business expenses) is amended by inserting after
 subsection (h) the following new subsection:
 `(i) EXCESSIVE COMPENSATION-
 `(1) IN GENERAL- In the case of a domestic motor vehicle manufacturer,
 no deduction shall be allowed under subsection (a)(1) for any excessive
 compensation.
 `(2) EXCESSIVE COMPENSATION- For purposes of this subsection, the term
 `excessive compensation' means the amount by which--
 `(A) the compensation paid or incurred by an employer for the personal
 services of any employee for the taxable year, exceeds
 `(B) an amount equal to 25 times the lowest compensation paid or incurred
 by the employer for the personal services of any other employee for the
 taxable year.
 `(3) DEFINITIONS AND SPECIAL RULES- For purposes of this subsection--
 `(A) DOMESTIC MOTOR VEHICLE MANUFACTURER- The term `domestic motor vehicle
 manufacturer' means a domestic corporation that manufactures motor vehicles
 in one or more production facilities located in the United States. Such
 term does not include any reporting corporation (within the meaning of
 section 6038A).
 `(B) COMPENSATION-
 `(i) IN GENERAL- The term `compensation' means salary, wages, and bonuses.
 `(ii) PART-YEAR EMPLOYEES- In the case of any part-year employee, the
 compensation of the employee shall be computed on an annualized basis.
 `(C) EMPLOYER- All persons treated as a single employer under subsection
 (a) or (b) of section 52 or subsection (m) or (o) of section 414 shall be
 treated as 1 employer.
 `(D) EMPLOYEE- The term `employee' means a full-time permanent employee.'
 (b) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendment made by this section shall apply to
 taxable years beginning after December 31, 1995.
