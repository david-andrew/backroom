A BILL
To amend the Federal Deposit Insurance Act to regulate certain marketing
activities engaged in on the premises of deposit-taking facilities of insured
depository institutions.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Depositor Protection and Fraud Act of 1990'.
SEC. 2. REGULATION OF CERTAIN NONDEPOSIT MARKETING ACTIVITIES IN PUBLIC
AREAS OF RETAIL BRANCHES OF INSURED DEPOSITORY INSTITUTIONS.
 Section 18 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 1828) is amended
 by adding at the end the following new subsection:
 `(o) REGULATION OF CERTAIN NONDEPOSIT MARKETING ACTIVITIES-
 `(1) PROHIBITION ON CERTAIN ACTIVITIES ON BEHALF OF AFFILIATES- No insured
 depository institution may permit any evidence of indebtedness of, or
 ownership interest in, any affiliate (of such depository institution) to
 be sold or offered for sale in any part of any office of such institution
 which is commonly accessible to the general public for the purpose of
 accepting deposits.
 `(2) PROHIBITION ON SALE OF CERTAIN INSTRUMENTS BY DEPOSITORY INSTITUTIONS-
 No insured depository institution may sell or offer for sale, in any
 part of any office of such institution which is commonly accessible to
 the general public for the purpose of accepting deposits, any evidence of
 indebtedness of, or ownership interest in, such institution if any proceeds
 of such sale may qualify as, or be treated as, capital of the institution
 under any provision of Federal law or pursuant to regulations prescribed
 by the appropriate Federal banking agency.
 (3) DISCRETIONARY AUTHORITY TO PROHIBIT OR IMPOSE CONDITIONS ON OTHER SALES
 BY DEPOSITORY INSTITUTIONS- The appropriate Federal banking agency may,
 by regulation or order, prohibit or impose any condition with respect to
 any sale, or any offer to sell, by any insured depository institution of--
 `(A) any evidence of indebtedness of, or ownership interest in, such
 institution or any affiliate of such institution (which is not otherwise
 subject to the prohibition contained in paragraph (1) or (2)); or
 `(B) any type of indebtedness or ownership interest referred to in
 subparagraph (A),
in any part of any office of the institution which is commonly accessible to
the general public for the purpose of accepting deposits if the appropriate
Federal banking agency determines that such evidence of indebtedness of,
or ownership interest in, the institution is likely to be confused by the
general public with an insured deposit.
 `(4) EXCEPTION FOR DEPOSITS AND CERTAIN MEANS OF PAYMENT TO 3D PARTIES-
 Paragraphs (1), (2), and (3) shall not apply with respect to any evidence
 of indebtedness which--
 `(A) is a deposit in an insured depository institution; or
 `(B) constitutes a means of payment to a third party, such as a traveler's
 check, cashier's check, teller's check, certified check, or money order.'.
