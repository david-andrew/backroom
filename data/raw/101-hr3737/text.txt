A BILL
To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to impose an excise tax on certain
uses of virgin materials and to establish a trust fund for recycling assistance
and solid waste management planning.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. EXCISE TAX ON USE OF VIRGIN MATERIALS.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Chapter 38 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to
 environmental taxes) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following
 new subchapter:
`Subchapter D--Use of Virgin Materials.
`Sec. 4681. Imposition of tax.
`Sec. 4682. Definition and special rules.
`SEC. 4681. IMPOSITION OF TAX.
 `(a) GENERAL RULE- There is hereby imposed a tax on the use of any virgin
 material in the manufacture or production of any taxable product.
 `(b) AMOUNT OF TAX-
 `(1) IN GENERAL- The amount of the tax imposed by subsection (a) shall be
 $7.50 per ton of virgin material which is used as described in subsection
 (a).
 `(2) PHASE-IN OF TAX- In the case of a use during a calendar year before
 1992, paragraph (1) shall be applied by substituting the amount determined
 under the following table for `$7.50':

 `(c) LIABILITY FOR TAX- The tax imposed by subsection (a) shall be paid
 by the person using the virgin material as described in subsection (a).
`SEC. 4682. DEFINITION AND SPECIAL RULES.
 `(a) TAXABLE PRODUCT- For purposes of this subchapter, the term `taxable
 product' means any product manufactured or produced by an industry described
 in any of the following Standard Industrial Classification Codes:
 `(1) 2621 (paper mills other than building paper).
 `(2) 2631 (paperboard mills).
 `(3) 2643 (bags other than textile bags).
 `(4) 2648 (stationery, tablets, and related products).
 `(5) 2654 (sanitary food containers).
 `(6) 2821 (plastics materials, synthetic resins, and nonvulcanizable
 elastomers).
 `(7) 3079 (miscellaneous plastics products).
 `(8) 3221 (glass containers).
 `(9) 3353 (aluminum sheet, plate, and foil).
 `(10) 3411 (metal cans).
 `(11) 3466 (crowns and closures).
 `(12) 3692 (primary batteries, whether wet or dry).
 `(b) Special Rules-
 `(1) FRACTIONAL PARTS OF A TON- In the case of a fraction of a ton, the
 tax imposed by section 4681 shall be the same fraction of the amount of
 such tax imposed on a whole ton.
 `(2) DISPOSITION OF REVENUES FROM PUERTO RICO AND THE VIRGIN ISLANDS-
 The provisions of subsections (a)(3) and (b)(3) of section 7652 shall not
 apply to any tax imposed by section 4681.'
 (b) CLERICAL AMENDMENT- The table of subchapters for chapter 38 of such
 Code is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new item:
`Subchapter D. Use of virgin materials.'
 (c) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendments made by this section shall take effect
 on January 1, 1990.
SEC. 2. RECYCLING ASSISTANCE AND SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT PLANNING TRUST FUND.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Subchapter A of chapter 98 of the Internal Revenue Code of
 1986 (relating to trust fund code) is amended by adding at the end thereof
 the following new section:
`SEC. 9511. RECYCLING ASSISTANCE AND SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT PLANNING TRUST
FUND.
 `(a) CREATION OF TRUST FUND- There is established in the Treasury of the
 United States a trust fund to be known as the `Recycling Assistance and
 Solid Waste Management Planning Trust Fund', consisting of such amounts
 as may be appropriated or credited to such Trust Fund as provided in this
 section or section 9602(b).
 `(b) TRANSFERS TO TRUST FUND- There is hereby appropriated to the Recycling
 Assistance and Solid Waste Management Planning Trust Fund the amount
 determined by the Secretary to be equivalent to the taxes received in the
 Treasury under section 4681 (relating to tax on use of virgin materials).
 `(c) EXPENDITURES FROM TRUST FUND- Amounts in the Recycling Assistance and
 Solid Waste Management Planning Trust Fund shall be available, as provided
 in appropriation Acts--
 `(1) to provide financial assistance pursuant to section 7011 of the Solid
 Waste Disposal Act; and
 `(2) to support the waste reduction and recycling clearinghouse established
 pursuant to section 8008 of the Solid Waste Disposal Act.'
 (b) CLERICAL AMENDMENT- The table of sections for such subchapter is
 amended by adding at the end thereof the following new item:
`Sec. 9511. Recycling Assistance and Solid Waste Management Planning Trust
Fund.'
