A BILL
Regarding the importation of Japanese motor vehicles into the United States.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as `The Two-Way Street Act'.
SEC. 2. DEFINITIONS.
 As used in this Act:
 (1) The term `base period' means, with respect to any calendar year after
 1992, the period of 12 consecutive months ending on June 30 preceding the
 commencement of such calendar year.
 (2) The term `motor vehicle' means any article of a kind described in
 heading 8703 or 8704 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States.
SEC. 3. QUANTITATIVE LIMITATIONS ON THE IMPORTATION OF JAPANESE MOTOR VEHICLES.
 The aggregate quantity of motor vehicles that are products of Japan that
 may be entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, in the customs
 territory of the United States during any calendar year after 1992 may
 not exceed the aggregate quantity of motor vehicles that--
 (1) are products of the United States; and
 (2) were exported to, and sold at retail in, Japan during the base period
 for such year.
SEC. 4. ADMINISTRATION.
 The Secretary of Commerce, on a timely basis, shall compute, and notify
 the Commissioner of the United States Customs Service of, the quantitative
 limitation that is applicable under section 3 for each calendar year
 after 1992.
