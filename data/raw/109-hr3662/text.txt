 A BILL

To delay for 2 years the general effective date of the Bankruptcy Abuse 
 Prevention and Consumer Protection Act of 2005.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Financial Safeguards for Hurricane 
Survivors Act of 2005''.

SEC. 2. AMENDMENT TO BANKRUPTCY ABUSE PREVENTION AND CONSUMER 
 PROTECTION ACT OF 2005.

 Section 1501(a) of the Bankruptcy Abuse Prevention and Consumer 
Protection Act of 2005 (Public Law 109-8) is amended by striking ``180 
days'' and inserting ``910 days''.

SEC. 3. EFFECTIVE DATE.

 This Act and the amendment by this Act shall take effect of 
September 1, 2005.
