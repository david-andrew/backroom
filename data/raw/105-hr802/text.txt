 A BILL

 To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the estate and 
 gift taxes.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPEAL OF ESTATE AND GIFT TAXES.

 (a) Estate Tax.--Chapter 11 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
(relating to estate taxes) is hereby repealed.
 (b) Gift Tax.--Chapter 12 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
(relating to gift taxes) is hereby repealed.
 (c) Effective Date.--
 (1) Estate tax.--The repeal made by subsection (a) shall 
 apply to the estates of decedents dying after September 30, 
 1997.
 (2) Gift tax.--The repeal made by subsection (b) shall 
 apply with respect to transfers made after September 30, 1997.
