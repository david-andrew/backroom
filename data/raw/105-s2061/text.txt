 A BILL

To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to prohibit transfers or 
 discharges of residents of nursing facilities.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. RESTRICTIONS ON TRANSFERS OR DISCHARGES OF NURSING FACILITY 
 RESIDENTS UNDER THE MEDICAID PROGRAM.

 (a) In General.--Section 1919(d) of the Social Security Act (42 
U.S.C. 1396r(d)) is amended--
 (1) by redesignating paragraph (4) as paragraph (5); and
 (2) by inserting after paragraph (3), the following:
 ``(4) Restrictions on transfers or discharges of 
 residents.--
 ``(A) In general.--A nursing facility shall not--
 ``(i) transfer or discharge (or seek to 
 transfer or discharge) from the facility any 
 resident on the basis of the resident's 
 eligibility for medical assistance for services 
 provided by the facility (and for having 
 payment made) under the State plan under this 
 title; and
 ``(ii) if the facility ceases or will cease 
 to be a participating provider under the State 
 plan under this title, transfer or discharge 
 (or seek to transfer or discharge) from the 
 facility any resident on the basis of the 
 resident's eligibility for medical assistance 
 under the State plan with respect to such 
 services.
 ``(B) Continuation of payments and oversight 
 authority.--Notwithstanding any other provision of this 
 title, a facility described in subparagraph (A)(ii) 
 shall be deemed to be a participating provider under 
 the State plan under this title with respect to the 
 residents determined to be eligible for medical 
 assistance under the State plan who remain in the 
 facility after the facility otherwise ceases to be a 
 participating provider under the State plan for 
 purposes of--
 ``(i) receiving payments under the State 
 plan for services provided to such residents; 
 and
 ``(ii) maintaining compliance with all 
 applicable requirements of this title.
 ``(C) New residents.--This paragraph shall not 
 apply to or affect a facility's refusal to accept any 
 individual as a new resident of the facility.''.
 (b) Clarification Regarding Transfers or Discharges for Failure To 
Pay.--Section 1919(c)(2)(A) of such Act (42 U.S.C. 1396r(c)(2)(A)) is 
amended, in the matter following clause (vi), by inserting before the 
period the following: ``, and in no case shall the withdrawal or 
termination of the facility as a participating provider under the State 
plan under this title be construed as a failure to pay on the part of a 
resident who has been determined to be eligible for medical assistance 
for services provided by the facility under the State plan''.
 (c) Effective Date.--The amendments made by this section shall 
apply to transfers or discharges occurring on or after the date of 
enactment of this section.
