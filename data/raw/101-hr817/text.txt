A BILL
To amend the Federal Aviation Act of 1958 to make permanent the prohibition
against smoking on scheduled domestic flights of 2 hours or less in duration
and to extend such prohibition to all scheduled domestic flights.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SMOKING BAN.
 Section 404(d) of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958 (49 U.S.C. App. 1374(d))
 is amended by striking out the subsection heading and paragraph (1) and
 inserting in lieu thereof the following:
 `(d) PROHIBITION AGAINST SMOKING ON SCHEDULED FLIGHTS AND TAMPERING WITH
 SMOKE ALARM DEVICES-
 `(1) SMOKING PROHIBITION-
 `(A) GENERAL RULE- It shall be unlawful to smoke in the passenger cabin
 or lavatory on any scheduled airline flight in intrastate, interstate,
 or overseas air transportation.
 `(B) REGULATIONS- The Secretary of Transportation shall issue such
 regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this
 subsection.
 `(C) LIMITATION ON APPLICABILITY- Subparagraph (A) shall only apply to
 a flight--
 `(i) in calendar year 1989 if such flight is scheduled for 2 hours or less
 in duration;
 `(ii) in calendar year 1990 if such flight is scheduled for 3 hours or
 less in duration; and
 `(iii) in calendar year 1991 if such flight is scheduled for 4 hours or
 less in duration.'.
SEC. 2. CONFORMING AMENDMENT.
 Paragraph (2) of subsection (d) of section 404 of the Federal Aviation Act
 of 1958 (49 U.S.C. App. 1374(d)) is amended by inserting `TAMPERING WITH
 SMOKE ALARM DEVICES- ' before `Any passenger' and by aligning such paragraph
 with paragraph (1) of such subsection as amended by section 1 of this Act.
