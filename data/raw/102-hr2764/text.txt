Mr. RAMSTAD introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee
on Education and Labor
A BILL
To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to provide that an employee
shall not be paid overtime compensation for overtime hours worked without
authorization.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. OVERTIME RESTRICTION.
 Section 7 of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 207) is
 amended by adding at the end the following:
 `(r) An employer shall not be required to pay the overtime compensation
 prescribed by subsection (a) to an employee for hours worked in excess of
 forty in any workweek if such hours were worked without the authorization
 of the employer.'.
SEC. 2. EFFECTIVE DATE.
 The amendment made by section 1 shall take effect 180 days after the date
 of the enactment of this Act.
