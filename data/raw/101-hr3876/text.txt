A BILL
To preserve and maintain as housing affordable to low-income families or
persons privately owned dwellings that were produced for such purpose with
Federal assistance.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
 This Act may be cited as the `Low-Income Housing Preservation Act of 1990'.
SEC. 2. PERMANENT EXTENSION OF LOW-INCOME HOUSING MORTGAGE PREPAYMENT
LIMITATION.
 Section 203 of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1987 (12
 U.S.C. 17151 note) is repealed.
SEC. 3. GRANTS TO PUBLIC AGENCIES AND NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS FOR LOW-INCOME
HOUSING PRESERVATION.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Subtitle D of title II of the Housing and Community
 Development Act of 1987 (42 U.S.C. 17151 note) is amended by adding at
 the end the following new section:
`SEC. 264. GRANTS FOR LOW-INCOME HOUSING PRESERVATION.
 `(a) IN GENERAL- The Secretary of Housing and Urban Development may make
 grants to public agencies and nonprofit organizations for the purpose of
 assisting the agencies and organizations in acquiring eligible low-income
 housing.
 `(b) REQUIREMENTS- Grants under this section may only be used to acquire
 eligible low-income housing--
 `(1) that will be retained as housing affordable for very low-income families
 or persons, lower income families or persons, and moderate-income families
 or persons for the remaining useful life of the property and
 `(2) for which the conditions of subparagraphs (B) through (F) of section
 225(b)(3) will be fulfilled.
 `(c) PREFERENCES- In awarding grants under this section, the Secretary
 shall give preference to nonprofit organizations and public agencies that,
 in the determination of the Secretary, will provide housing for greater
 proportions of very low and lower income families and persons and that
 will provide longer terms of very low-, lower, and moderate income use.
 `(d) DEFINITIONS- For purposes of this section:
 `(1) ELIGIBLE LOW-INCOME HOUSING- The term eligible low-income housing
 shall have the meaning given the term in section 233(l).
 `(2) NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION- The term nonprofit organization means a
 private organization (including a limited equity cooperative)--
 `(A) no part of the net earnings of which inures to the benefit of any
 member, shareholder, founder, contributor, or individual; and
 `(B) that is approved by the Secretary as to financial responsibility.
 `(3) PUBLIC AGENCY- The term public agency--
 `(A) means any State, local, or other governmental entity (not including
 any entity of the Federal Government); and
 `(B) includes any public housing agency.
 `(4) SECRETARY- The term Secretary means the Secretary of Housing and
 Urban Development.
 `(5) VERY LOW-, LOWER, AND MODERATE INCOME FAMILIES OR PERSONS- The terms
 very low-income families or persons, lower income families or persons,
 and moderate income families or persons shall have the meaning given the
 terms in section 233.
 `(e) REGULATIONS- The Secretary shall issue any regulations necessary to
 carry out this section.
 `(f) AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS- There are authorized to be appropriated
 to carry out this section $300,000,000.
 `(g) CONFORMING AMENDMENT- The table of contents for subtitle D of title
 II of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1987 in section 1(b)
 of such Act (42 U.S.C. 5301 note) is amended by inserting after the item
 relating to section 263 the following new item:
`Sec. 264. Grants for low-income housing preservation.'.
SEC. 4. EXCLUSION OF GAIN ON SALES OF QUALIFIED LOW INCOME HOUSING.
 (a) IN GENERAL- Part III of subchapter B of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue
 Code of 1986 (relating to items specifically excluded from gross income)
 is amended by redesignating section 136 as section 137 and by inserting
 after section 135 the following new section:
`SECTION. 136. GAIN ON SALES OF QUALIFIED LOW-INCOME HOUSING.
 `(a) GENERAL RULE- Gross income does not include any gain from the sale
 or exchange of qualified low-income housing.
 `(b) QUALIFIED LOW-INCOME HOUSING- For purposes of this section, the term
 qualified low-income housing means housing which is sold or exchanged
 pursuant to a plan of action--
 `(1) which is approved by the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development
 under subtitle B of the Emergency Low-Income Housing Preservation Act of
 1987 (12 U.S.C. 17151 note),
 `(2) which extends the low-income affordability restrictions applicable
 to such housing for the remaining useful life of the buildings used for
 such housing, and
 `(3) for which the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development has entered
 into an agreement under section 224(b)(7) of such Act to apply the exclusion
 under this section.'
 `(b) CONFORMING AMENDMENT- The table of sections for part III of subchapter
 B of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking
 the item relating to section 136 and inserting the following:
`Sec. 136. Gain on sales of qualified low-income housing.
`Sec. 137. Cross references to other Acts.'.
 (c) EFFECTIVE DATE- The amendments made by this section shall apply to
 sales or exchanges after the date of the enactment of this Act, in taxable
 years ending after such date.
