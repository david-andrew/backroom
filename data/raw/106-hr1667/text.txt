 A BILL

 To amend title 23, United States Code, relating to vehicle weight 
 limitations.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

 This Act may be cited as the ``Safe and Efficient Transportation 
Act''.

SEC. 2. VEHICLE WEIGHT LIMITATIONS.

 Section 127 of title 23, United States Code, is amended by adding 
at the end the following:
 ``(h) Exception.--Notwithstanding subsection (a), a State may allow 
a combination of a truck tractor and no more than 1 trailer or 
semitrailer with a consecutive set of tandem and tridem axles that may 
carry a gross load of 34,000 pounds on the tandem axles and 51,000 
pounds on the tridem axles to use the Interstate System if the overall 
trailer length is at least 48 feet; except that such overall gross 
weight may not exceed 97,000 pounds for a vehicle combination of 6 or 
more axles (for a combination of a truck tractor and no more than 1 
trailer or semitrailer), including all enforcement tolerances.''.
