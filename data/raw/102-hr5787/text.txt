 H. R. 5787
To repeal the Service Contract Act of 1965.
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
August 6, 1992
Mr. ALLEN (for himself, Mr. ROHRABACHER, Mr. DANNEMEYER, Mr. SMITH of Texas,
Mr. COBLE, and Mr. BOEHNER) introduced the following bill; which was referred
to the Committee on Education and Labor
A BILL
To repeal the Service Contract Act of 1965.
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United
 States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. REPEAL OF SERVICE CONTRACT ACT OF 1965.
 (a) REPEAL- The Service Contract Act of 1965 (41 U.S.C. 351 et seq.) is
 repealed.
 (b) SAVINGS- Notwithstanding subsection (a), the Service Contract Act of 1965
 (41 U.S.C. 351 et seq.), as in effect before the date of the enactment of
 this Act, shall apply to a contract entered into before the date of the
 enactment of this Act as if this section were not enacted.
