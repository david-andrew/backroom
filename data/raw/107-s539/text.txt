 A BILL

 To amend the Truth in Lending Act to prohibit finance charges for on-
 time payments.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PROHIBITION ON FINANCE CHARGES FOR ON-TIME PAYMENTS.

 Section 27 of the Truth in Lending Act (15 U.S.C. 1637) is amended 
by adding at the end the following:
 ``(h) Prohibition on Finance Charges for On-Time Payments.--In the 
case of any credit card account under an open end consumer credit plan, 
where no other balance is owing on the account, no finance or interest 
charge may be imposed with regard to any amount of a new extension of 
credit that was paid on or before the date on which it was due.''.
